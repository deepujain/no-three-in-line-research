## Exact decomposition

Deleting selected rows r,s,t partitions every completed conflict triple by the number of new points: zero gives retained U_R; one gives the three retained-pair unary sums; two gives the three retained-point cross sums; three gives the ternary determinant table. A triple containing two distinct points of one selected row cannot also contain an off-row point, because its line is horizontal. Thus the displayed objective is exhaustive and disjoint under the enforced row loads. Equal columns across rows remain legal: the pair cross tables must include their diagonals, and T[a,a,a]=1 counts vertical all-new triples. Multiple retained collinear points contribute separately to C, and larger-line multiplicities are exactly accounted for by the binomial identity in the proposal. Omitting T would invalidate the method. The stated static n3 falsifier exhibits that omission algebraically without a computation claim.

The complete original pair-line index contains every line with at least two retained points. Masking the three selected rows therefore suffices to derive retained U_R and insertion unary costs, even when a line loses all or most of its original points. Exact signed divisibility at row intersections avoids geometric rounding. Horizontal retained lines cannot meet a distinct selected row, so omitting their unary intersections is correct. The independent tiny review must still verify implementation of these identities rather than trust this design proof.

For current conflicts, I, J and K count incidences with selected row events. Inclusion/exclusion gives M=I_r+I_s+I_t-J_rs-J_rt-J_st+K_rst, the exact number of old triples removed, equivalently U-U_R. Row capacity ensures three different rows in every old violating triple. K may have multiplicity from different lines with the same row labels, and sum(K)=U, so positive K keys number at most U≤65. Restricting eligibility to K>0 and ordering by (-M,r,s,t) is deterministic but heuristic: K=0 blocks may improve, and M does not establish an achievable minimum.

## Legal restricted neighborhoods and ties

Every row domain retains its current columns, making the current assignment eligible even when those unary costs are high. Filling to four by ordered missing unary minima defines a state/block-specific finite neighborhood. Total six selected points forces 2/2/2. Total five permits exactly 1/2/2,2/1/2,2/2/1; all three must be considered so the singleton can migrate within the selected block. These replacements preserve149 distinct cells and exactly one singleton globally, and they preserve every unselected point. The sorted set choices exclude duplicate columns within a row.

At four columns per row, total six has6^3=216 assignments; total five has3·4·6·6=432. The proposed merged singleton/pair X sequence, then compatible merged Y sequence and fixed-size Z sequence, enumerates the full legal union in global (X,Y,Z) tuple order. Scanning orientations in separate batches would not preserve that tie order. All objective summands are nonnegative integers, so the first fully scored zero in the specified order is a globally lexical minimum within this restricted domain even if enumeration is not exhausted. A positive partial best does not establish an optimum. On exhaustive nonimprovement the minimum must equal current U; a greater value contradicts current eligibility, and a lower value is a strict move. Checking the current formula first and rebuilding any positive accepted state are appropriate independent safeguards.

## State and charge accounting

The proposed controller is solely the new restricted three-row family, with16 total starts across all states. A durable start precedes domain/table/enumeration work. A pending interrupted start consumes its charge and covers nothing. Nonimprovement advances only the bound current-state cursor. A strict acceptance installs the state and resets current coverage/domain/ranking identity together; the same row labels on a new state define a new neighborhood. No row or pair followup, extra chunk, retry, domain widening or restart is implicit. Since every accept is attached to a start, at most16 accepts and17 active states are possible. Old row/pair counts remain provenance rather than active allowances.

The artifact-before-reference and single authoritative snapshot design preserves the distinction between a saved witness and a successfully adopted zero state. Saved zero geometry may survive later metadata or publication failure while the durable authority remains positive and pending; overall acceptance must still fail. This review endorses the specified mathematical boundaries, not an unimplemented filesystem or validator lifecycle.
