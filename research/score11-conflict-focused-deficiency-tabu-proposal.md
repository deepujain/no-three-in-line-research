# Conflict-focused sampled tabu walk on direct 157-point states

Status: design only. No implementation, n=79 scoring or numerical search was performed for this proposal. A separate mathematical design review, source/tiny review and lead-owned allocation are required. No observed effectiveness or probability is asserted.

## Motivation and scope

The audited direct-157 seed A is the fixed 158-point S minus (78,78), with five collinear triples and one deficient row and column. The accepted one-neighborhood census found no nonworsening move, and the three-exit two-step census found no score below five within peak six; the latter's full histograms rely on the reviewed execution and were not independently replayed. Thus a greedy non-increasing single-move walk is stuck at A. A previous 100,000-proposal 157 walk sampled relocations and rectangles uniformly and used fixed-temperature 2 Metropolis acceptance; it did not improve five. Its actual run and the two-step result are limited observations, not global evidence.

This proposal changes the 157 move-selection policy: direct conflict incidence biases a small candidate sample, a best-admissible selection deliberately chooses an uphill move if every sampled move worsens the score, and a bounded recent-state tabu list discourages immediate reversal. It does not replace the 157 representation with two full permutations. Existing `src/trade_search.py` already has conflict focus, tabu and cooling for 158-point degree-two states, so neither focus nor tabu is novel. The proposed combination applies them to the previously reviewed deficient-axis representation, with sampled best-admissible selection rather than Metropolis and without the 158 permutation/decomposition requirement. It is also not the valid-prefix sixteen-cycle rebuild, or an exhaustive neighborhood census. No result from one method proves behavior of the other.

## One deterministic bounded policy

Pin the accepted A certificate, its independent actual audit and fixed S, including byte and normalized identities. Before search verify exact 157 distinct in-bounds cells, one degree-one row/column and all other axis degrees two, A subset S with one deletion, exact initial score five, and matching conflict-coordinate lists and hashes from both full validators. Pin the reviewed `research/score11_deficiency_walk.py`, its exact `point_conflicts` and `rectangle_proposal` dependencies, verifier, candidate implementation, tests and guard. Existing frozen files and historical receipts remain unchanged.

Use local `random.Random(279179)` and a fixed maximum of 2,000 decision steps, 32 sampled proposal attempts per step, and no restart, checkpoint continuation, reseeding or alternate temperature. A decision step begins only before the deadline. Recompute all current exact conflict triples in canonical sorted point-coordinate order at the start of each step; assert their count equals the incremental score before sampling. If enumeration is interrupted or incomplete, do not use it as an exact list or score. Form the list of distinct points appearing in at least one current triple, sorted lexicographically. If it is empty, independently validate and immediately save the zero-score witness; never choose a random focus point from an empty list.

For each of the first eight samples choose one conflict point uniformly from that sorted list and try its row relocation; for the next eight choose a conflict point independently and try its column relocation; for the last sixteen independently choose a conflict point and a second occupied point uniformly from the sorted current points, then try the uncolored rectangle switch. Sampling is with replacement; illegal proposals and repeated resulting sets count as attempts but are not candidates. Use the reviewed wrappers and direct membership/bounds checks. A sampled move need not eliminate the chosen conflict, and a state may receive no legal sampled move.

Compute each legal move's exact integer delta using sequential point removal/addition and evaluate the resulting score. Rank distinct proposed point sets by (resulting score, sorted resulting coordinates), after rejecting those in a FIFO tabu of the last 64 accepted point sets, including the current set; a tabu candidate that is strictly better than the globally best exact score is aspirationally eligible. With exact full-state keys and a monotone best over all accepted states, this aspiration is unreachable in a correct run because every tabu state was already visited; it is not an expected escape mechanism. Choose the minimum of the admissible sample and accept it unconditionally, even if worse than the current score. Add the accepted set to the FIFO, evicting the oldest at capacity. Maintain a separate best-state record, never regress it. If no admissible candidate appears in a complete sample, stop with `no_admissible_sample`; do not remove tabu entries to force a move or automatically resample. Each accepted transition keeps exactly 157 points and the deficient-axis pattern; it may relocate the deficiency, have any positive conflict score, and may move toward or away from S. No intrinsic source-deletion bound applies to transient states. One sampled step is not a complete neighborhood examination.

Use the same seed and exact sample order on the same implementation and runtime. A wall-clock stop changes the number of completed steps and does not promise bitwise reproducibility across Python/platform versions. Record proposed/illegal/duplicate/tabu/admissible counts by class, fully completed versus interrupted steps, an accepted-score histogram with at most 2,000 entries and uphill/neutral/downhill totals, cache capacity/evictions, best verified score and precise stop reason. Do not record an unbounded history: store only current, best, 64 bounded tabu keys and bounded counters.

## Artifacts and outcome criteria

Save the initial state as the validated score-five incumbent. On each strict best score decrease, independently validate the exact candidate with both the normalized-slope and cross-product validators, matching complete conflict-coordinate lists, normalized identity, 157-point count and axis degrees. Save atomically before continuing; positive-score candidates remain invalid research states. A score below five is a meaningful scoped diagnostic even when it is not a witness, not an improved lower bound. A zero score is a 157-point witness only after both validators pass; immediately persist it and stop, even if the soft deadline passes during validator two. If validator one finishes after the deadline, do not start validator two; a partial validation is not an exact saved result.

Separately, on first reaching effective source distance |S minus current| >= 13, dual-validate and save that exact state once as a distance-exit diagnostic, even if its score is worse than five. Record its exact conflict score and source distance, and label positive scores invalid. This is a source-distance observation only, not evidence of improvement, validity or that a closed family was exhaustively escaped. A valid 157 endpoint with at least 13 deletions lies outside the prior native D<=8 scope and the separately attempted D=9..12 range, whose search timed out. Source-distance checks must use set difference, not number of moves or touched cells. Strict-best and first distance exit may be the same state: validate that candidate once and reuse the completed reports for every applicable label. A zero-score witness takes precedence: persist it first immediately after the second validator even if the deadline has expired; do not repeat validation or require an optional diagnostic before saving it. A single atomic candidate artifact may carry multiple applicable labels, and each report pointer must name the same verified identity.

## Proposed gate and validation

One future run, only after separate approval, uses a single 25-second monotonic soft deadline measured at process entry inside the existing 30-second / sampled 256-MiB guard. A 2,000-step cap bounds proposal attempts at 64,000; conflict enumeration, comparisons and validators are additional cost. These are operation limits, not a runtime or memory forecast. Check the clock inside triple enumeration and before proposals, incidence scoring and validators. Count started/completed candidate evaluations and steps; if a deadline cuts a sample short, record partial and make no selection from an incomplete sample. Atomic writes and full validators may overrun the cooperative deadline; the outer guard can interrupt them and its RSS measurement is sampled. Recheck all input and source pins before report publication; changed identity fails closed. No further gate or automatic retry follows either failure or no improvement.

A future proposed invocation, not currently executable until a separately reviewed implementation exists, is `.venv/bin/python research/score11_conflict_focused_tabu.py --output-dir data/score11-conflict-focused-tabu-v1 --seed 279179 --seconds 25 --max-steps 2000`, wrapped by the already reviewed owned guard. This is not authorization to run it.

Independent mathematical and source/tiny reviewers should test every legal relocation/rectangle delta and axis invariant against direct determinants, exact current conflict incidence including four-point lines, sorted sampling and reproducible operation-capped prefixes, rejected duplicates/illegal moves, 64-entry tabu eviction and aspiration logic without suggesting aspiration can occur with consistent state/best data, obligatory uphill acceptance, no-admissible stopping, sampled-step timeout without partial selection, unchanged best on worse moves, direct source-distance thresholds including reinsertion, independent validation disagreement, late witness and positive-score labeling, mutation/final identity failure, and guard lifecycle.
