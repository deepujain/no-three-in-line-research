# Independent actual acceptance protocol: row-block pilot

This is an audit design, not a search, trajectory replay or continuation allocation. It applies after Root provides the completed pilot report, owned receipt, frozen manifest and shutdown artifacts. Source/tiny work must not read or score actual n75 states in advance. A zero or claimed witness requires a separate exact witness audit; the positive-score helper described here must refuse to promote one.

## Identity and runtime gate

Bind the expected manifest digest explicitly. Snapshot the report, receipt and manifest and recheck their hashes before returning. Require exact equality between normalized manifest entries plus the manifest's own digest, worker report pins and receipt `sources_before`; reject aliases, missing worker/verifier/interpreter/stdlib-RNG pins, self-entry and live drift. Require worker hash `ee8a78e0f199f2a2f52e2bc37530c12a05c6b54a978f31371b05d9558cd51e0f`, the exact project virtualenv command with `--seconds 25`, the exact relative output/manifest paths and no extra argument or different order.

The owned receipt must be completed with unchanged sources, exited zero, clean cleanup, no monitor/cleanup/trigger error,30-second/256MiB limits and positive RSS/cleanup sampling. Guard integer fields must reject booleans. Elapsed values must be finite, worker time below25, guard time in(0,30), outer time at most30 and consistently nested. Require accepted/identity-valid checkpoint status and `closure:false`; normal budget termination accepts only a saved prefix, never a mathematical family conclusion.

## Checkpoint and state verification

Bind `completed-boundary.json` to the report's exact size/hash, enforce2MiB total and16KiB metadata, exact top-level schema, fixed n75/k149/seed275149/policy, actual23/25 budgets and runtime/RNG source identities. Parse exact integer coordinates and149 distinct cells in0..74, with one row of load one and74 rows of load two. Verify deficient row and normalized current/best point hashes. Column overload is allowed for these invalid research states and must not be silently repaired or treated as a witness.

Require canonical strictly ordered nondefault `[a,b,c,w]` records, at most65536, primitive sign convention and coefficient bounds,2<=w<=65535. Validate exact RNG version3 structure with624 unsigned32-bit words, index0..624 and null Gaussian cache, and confirm pinned-runtime `setstate` accepts it without drawing. This validates a state receipt, not that its RNG position follows from the whole recorded trajectory.

Logical counters must be unsigned64-bit and consistent: row+migration transitions equal completed transitions, completed migrations equal floor(T/16), raw up+neutral+down equals T<=200000, penalty events at most floor(T/32), best-improvement counters bounded, stagnation0..31 and capture epoch no later than total penalties. Every weight is at most penalty_events+1 because it started at one and increments once per event. Validate all report/checkpoint score/counter/classification bindings; historical best-weighted-at-capture is bounded but cannot generally be recomputed from the final weight map.

Independently enumerate exact determinants of all C(149,3)=540274 triples for initial, current and best states. Compute current weighted score by assigning each actual collinear triple its canonical line weight, independently of the worker pair index. Require exact checkpoint/report raw and current weighted agreement, best<=current and best<=initial. These are independently verified saved-state facts. Require positive current and best scores for this helper and explicitly label them invalid research states. No source repair, search or neighborhood census occurs.

For the initial artifact, independently reproduce only the seed275149 initialization draws and compare the literal starting points; do not replay any transition. A positive completed pilot must retain its nonzero initial artifact. For an optional best diagnostic, require file/flag agreement, exact best identity, axis counts, claimed score, invalid classification, both saved validator algorithms/counts/identities/errors and the direct indexed-conflict-list hash. The production diagnostic score guard is1000. Snapshot and recheck auxiliary artifact hashes too.

## Progress metrics and limits of inference

Validate the bounded power-of-two and penalty checkpoint formats, completed-step ranges and deterministic sequence constraints. Enforce at most64 telemetry records combined and at most32 penalty records. Report the number of distinct recorded penalty-state hashes, repeated hashes, initial-to-best saved-state reduction, completed migration/penalty counters and reported post-penalty best-improvement count. Clearly label historical counters/hashes as reported telemetry whose schema is checked; do not claim their underlying intermediate states or chronology were independently replayed.

The initial/current/best determinant checks establish those saved endpoints and the reproducible initial baseline. They do not prove every intermediate move, penalty update, RNG transition, global minimum or complete trajectory. Sparse diversity cannot establish mixing, absence of cycles or success probability. A checkpoint alone does not authorize a production loader or exact continuation. Root assesses the pilot against the original continuation criteria; no unchanged replay or continued search is automatic.

The audit helper imports no production geometry/index/transition code, writes no search state, launches no process and runs only inside Root's separately allocated owned guard. Three endpoint audits require1620822 determinant tests; optional best-validator conflict-hash verification may add another bounded pass unless cached. Pin I/O and JSON/weight/RNG checks are additional bounded work. Actual timing/RSS must be measured; a failed or incomplete audit cannot accept the prefix. A valid witness discovered in any artifact must be diverted to its own full witness/provenance audit rather than treated as an invalid diagnostic.
