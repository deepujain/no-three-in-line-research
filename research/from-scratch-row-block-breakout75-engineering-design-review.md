## Exact costs and bounded data structures

The pair-line index is sufficient. Normalize each pair to primitive `(a,b,c)` with ax+by=c and first nonzero normal coordinate positive; OR the two point bits into that line's149-bit membership mask. Every line with at least two occupied points appears, with no missing pair support. There are at most11026 keys. A pass over bit counts computes exact U=sum C(t,3), W=sum w*C(t,3), and conflict-row incidence, including every triple on lines with t>=4. Horizontal masks have size at most two by the hard row invariant.

Masking a selected row from each index entry leaves all retained pair lines. Each nonhorizontal line contributes to at most one integer cell of the empty row, determined by exact signed divisibility of c-b*r by a. Its unary contribution is w*C(t,2). Two replacement points in that empty row share no triple with retained points, so their total cost is exactly additive. Candidate ranking may discard the common retained-state score; an exact delta is new unary sum minus the old row's unary sum. Enumerating2775 distinct unordered pairs or75 singletons handles forced uphill alternatives and uniform tied choice without a huge neighborhood store.

Deficiency migration must use separate sequential costs. For a removed point p, group all other points by their line through p; a group of size t contributes w*C(t,2) to removal. Then group retained points by lines through the destination q to compute insertion. This includes changed vertical capacity and all mixed interactions; reusing the same-row lemma would be wrong. Two temporary dictionaries of at most148 groups, one at a time, suffice. At32 attempts the conservative9536 point-line incidences fit the stated bound. Duplicate outcomes count as attempts but are ranked once; interrupted samples cannot select a candidate.

Build only one initial index and one proposed next index per attempted transition, reusing the committed index on the next decision. Do not additionally rebuild after a penalty update: its membership masks remain valid, and the weighted score can be independently recomputed from that same index and the staged map. The hard200000-attempt trajectory cap permits at most200001 full-or-partial index builds, each with at most11026 pair constructions. The design's2205211026 construction ceiling is correct. Remaining worst-case ceilings include roughly2.07billion row-index line visits,520.4million alternative-pair sums, and119.2million migration incidences; these deliberately loose totals are not a throughput claim. The23-second search deadline is the practical pilot bound.

Keep two current/next indexes, two weight maps only during a due penalty transaction, current/best/proposed149-point sets, bounded tie/migration records, one75-cell cost vector and bounded telemetry. At65536 weight keys, tuple/integer/dictionary overhead is material: conservatively budgeting tens of MiB for each weight map and several MiB per index, plus serialized checkpoint records/bytes and Python/validators, makes256MiB plausible. Copy-on-write dictionaries share existing immutable keys, but the measured synthetic envelope must include staging duplication and serialization; logical tuple counts alone do not establish RSS. Never precompute every board line or retain trajectory states.

All scores stay exact integers: U<=C(149,3)=540274 and W<=65535*540274=35406856590<2^36. A32-stagnant-event update must precheck every violated line and both caps, then stage all increments before commit; no key eviction, partial increment or stale weighted score is allowed. Stored nondefault weights are positive and at least two, preserving W=0 iff U=0.

## Transaction and checkpoint acceptance

The addendum supplies a sound single boundary. Candidate selection and its RNG draws, sample completion, next index/scores, best/stagnation updates and any penalty copy are provisional. One reference replacement commits the complete boundary. Precommit deadline/cap/error restores the previous RNG, points, weights, best and logical counters; attempted work remains separate telemetry. This also resolves cap-triggered selection: a chosen state is not counted accepted until the whole transaction completes. A completed zero index commits immediately without optional penalties, then receives validator/save priority before ordinary search work.

The shutdown-only checkpoint needs no serialized index or resume API. It records exact current/best points and scores, immutable weights, runtime-specific RNG version3 state, source/runtime/manifest identities and committed logical counters. The2MiB cap is justified with compact separators: canonical a is nonnegative and at most74, b has at most three characters, c at most six, and weight at most five. Each `[a,b,c,w]` record plus comma needs at most22 bytes, so65536 weights require at most1441793 bytes. The RNG, two small point arrays and16KiB metadata allowance leave ample headroom. Bound metadata before serialization; report and work counters are separate under the1MiB report cap. Build/size-check bytes before atomic publication and record their digest/size only after successful rename.

Search stops at23 seconds from entry; the remaining2 seconds belong to validation, identity checks and bounded shutdown in the same25-second soft envelope. The30-second owned guard remains necessary for native validators and I/O. Start neither validator after the main deadline; successful validator two is followed immediately by witness persistence, even when late. An unvalidated raw zero checkpoint is explicitly not a certificate. Later shutdown/pin failure clears run acceptance while retaining any earlier saved witness. A killed process may leave no checkpoint and cannot claim successful finalization.

## Falsifiers, distinction and authorization

Required independent tiny checks include exhaustive row-valid n2..4 states and every distinct row replacement, full determinant comparison with t>=4 lines, separate migration deltas/deficiency changes and a counterexample to two-row additivity; canonical slopes; complete tie/RNG ordering; forced uphill moves; penalty-induced ranking changes; best monotonicity; both caps without partial state; and empty alternative handling at n2. Lifecycle tests must interrupt selection/sample/index/penalty stages and compare points+weights+RNG+logical counters with the previous boundary, exercise completed-zero priority and late save, and perform a test-only split trajectory restored from its own tiny checkpoint. Production must have no checkpoint import/resume route. A separate synthetic allocation must stress65536 weights, two indexes/maps and serialization under the unchanged owned256MiB guard.

The proposal changes the construction mechanism from the prior source-seeded, hard-column, sampled relocation/rectangle tabu policies: it starts from scratch, permits overloaded columns, minimizes a full row exactly and uses persistent violated-line penalties.
