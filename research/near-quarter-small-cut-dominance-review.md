# Small-cut dominance and curtailed prefix evaluation

Decision: curtail the proposed six-prefix shadow evaluation before implementation or execution. The pure predicate remains mathematically correct, but it cannot add a rejection on these prefixes.

Root identified the decisive bound. The current optimistic row checks imply degree(v)>=d(v) in the radius multigraph: each positive-radius edge contributes one to its reflected row, and each central edge contributes two central-row points for one halved quotient demand. For any U, summing these inequalities gives d(U)<=2e(U)+|delta(U)|. Therefore the cut bound can add a failure only through outside demand: d(U)>2e(U)+B-d(U), or B<2d(U)-2e(U). With at most three vertices and each demand at most two, the right side is at most 12. Thus B>=12 makes every proposed two/three-vertex cut redundant after the old row checks. Since B is twice the number of remaining four-orbits, only at most five remaining orbits can offer added pruning.
