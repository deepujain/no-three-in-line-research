# Independent design review: conflict-focused deficient-state tabu walk

Decision: PASS for the revised bounded exploratory design, conditional on independent source/tiny review and Root's separate actual allocation. Reviewed proposal `research/score11-conflict-focused-deficiency-tabu-proposal.md`, SHA-256 `87159a71f951199a65132f9f2040ed5ae88bf8c6a5a0f19e2a991961e81eb198`. This review used source/document inspection and algebra only; no new n79 input scoring, candidate generation, conflict enumeration or search was performed.

The original proposal `8dbd804eb1b4abe642cdffcbe25fd2a095216ce0fd48d57c8372075996740edd` was clarified during review in three ways: full-state aspiration is explicitly unreachable with consistent exact-score history; accepted-score reporting is a bounded histogram rather than a trajectory; and one candidate's completed dual-validation reports are reused for coincident best/distance/witness labels, with immediate witness-first persistence. These clarifications are incorporated in the reviewed bytes and remove the corresponding ambiguities.

The state invariant and move deltas reuse the reviewed deficiency representation. Cardinality2n-1 and axis degrees at most2 imply exactly one degree1 row and one degree1 column. Moving an occupied cell from a full row into an absent cell in the deficient row preserves its column and moves the row deficiency; column relocation is the transpose. A legal uncolored rectangle toggles two occupied opposite corners to two absent crossed corners, preserving all axis degrees. Source/destination membership and distinctness must be established before calling the exact helper. Sequential deletions and insertions telescope the exact unordered-collinear-triple score, including triples involving multiple moved points and four-or-more-point lines. Neither geometry nor the source-retention scope is a move legality restriction here.

Recomputing the complete current conflict list before sampling checks the incremental score and supplies the exact focus population. The focus distribution is uniform over distinct points appearing in at least one conflict, not weighted by the number of incident conflicts. The eight row, eight column and sixteen rectangle attempts have the stated fixed order; rectangle second points are uniform over the whole occupied set and may coincide with the focus, producing an illegal attempt. Sampling is with replacement. An interrupted conflict enumeration cannot certify the score or provide a complete focus list. An interrupted32-attempt sample cannot select its provisional best candidate. This leaves any unvalidated sampled zero-score state unclaimed; only the specified complete selection and dual-validation path can promote a witness.

Canonical point-set identity makes duplicate suppression, FIFO membership and lexicographic tie-breaking precise. All unique admissible sample endpoints are ranked by exact score then sorted coordinates. Accepting the minimum unconditionally does permit an uphill transition when every admissible sampled endpoint is worse than the current state. It does not guarantee leaving a basin, reaching distant source states, visiting every neighbor, or finding a better score. `no_admissible_sample` means precisely one complete sample supplied no admissible endpoint; it is neither full-neighborhood exhaustion nor local optimality. There is no implicit tabu eviction, resample, restart or budget extension on that outcome.

For a tabu state T already accepted at some earlier step, its deterministic exact score C(T) is at least the current global best over all accepted states. Therefore C(T)<best is impossible in a consistent run; the aspiration branch cannot be an effective escape mechanism. Retaining the formal rule is harmless if the source/tests preserve this interpretation. Any observed real aspiration event would instead warrant an identity/score/best invariant investigation. With the FIFO initially containing A and capped at64 accepted identities, current membership and oldest-first eviction must remain exact; a state may become eligible again only after its key leaves the FIFO. The best artifact remains separate and never regresses on accepted worse moves.

The2,000 decision-step cap gives at most64,000 proposal attempts. Exact complete conflict enumeration, incidence computations, set comparisons and validators are additional work, so this bound does not predict runtime. A naive enumeration at each step could inspect C(157,3)=632710 triples per step; this is an arithmetic observation, not a request to implement or execute that work. Deadline checks inside enumeration and before proposals/scoring prevent partial results from being silently treated as complete; individual validator/hash/write calls still rely on the unchanged outer30-second/sampled256-MiB guard. The histogram has at most2,000 distinct accepted-score entries, the tabu has64 keys and the live sample at most32 distinct candidates; no trajectory or unbounded visited set is requested. One shared process-entry deadline must include initialization, initial saving, all steps and diagnostics.

For fixed S and endpoint P, effective source distance is D=|S minus P|. If |S|=158 and |P|=157, then D=|P minus S|+1. Reinsertions can decrease D, so move counts or touched cells are not substitutes. A first accepted current state with D>=13 is only a distance observation. Its positive score remains invalid and says nothing about feasibility beyond prior source-local solver scopes. A valid endpoint there would lie beyond the prior D<=8 negatives and the attempted9..12 range; the latter timed out and is not a proved closed family. A strict score decrease below5 is a scoped heuristic diagnostic, not an improved point-count bound.

Artifact precedence is coherent after revision. Initial A must first be independently saved with score5. Only a strict decrease can replace the best artifact. The first qualifying distance state is saved once, even if worse than5; sampled/rejected candidates do not count as reached current states. If a selected candidate qualifies for several labels, run the two validators once and use their same completed normalized identity for all labels. A dual-verified zero-score candidate is saved immediately before any optional diagnostic and stops the run, even when validator two crosses the soft deadline. If the deadline prevents validator two from starting, no new exact artifact is promoted. Report pointers and best/distance counters must update only after successful atomic publication. Final pin mutation fails acceptance closed; an earlier file's existence alone cannot override a failed final identity check.

The policy is distinct from the prior uniformT2 walk in focus distribution, best-of32 selection, unconditional acceptance and state tabu. Its primitive move support is the same relocation/rectangle graph; this is not a new completeness region or a long compound-cycle algorithm. It is also distinct from the preceding valid-prefix construction, whose retained constraints and eight root obstructions do not govern this invalid-state walk. Novelty and success probability claims are correctly absent.

Before actual allocation, source/tiny evidence should independently enumerate legal moves and direct determinant deltas on tiny deficiency states, including occupied deficient intersections and four-point lines. It should verify exact canonical conflict lists, scripted/RNG sample order, duplicate/illegal/tabu counting, unconditional uphill selection, lexicographic ties, FIFO64 eviction, consistent-history aspiration impossibility, empty-focus witness handling and no-admissible stopping. Fault tests should interrupt every phase of a representative complete step and confirm no selection from incomplete samples, no regression or false artifact pointer, shared step/time bounds and exact final pins. Artifact tests should combine strict-best/distance/witness labels, reuse dual reports, inject disagreement/write failure, save late validator-two witnesses, reject validator-two starts after deadline and track D under source reinsertion. No actual n79 performance or effectiveness follows from this design approval.
