It does not imply global n79 infeasibility. The actual depth-16 attempt stopped at its 200-orbit resource cap and supplied no verdict.

It is strictly stronger as a logical condition than the corresponding depth-17 no-good: Q is a proper subset of that retained set. It includes the diagonal literal; omitting it would require a separate argument covering other diagonal choices. A single Q result says nothing about other families or prefixes not containing Q.

This is a proposed semantic explanation, not a solver-produced minimal core. No claim of usefulness outside the already explored subtree is made: deterministic full-row branching can constrain where a subset is encountered, and no observed recurrence count or speedup is available. A SAT model, interrupted mask or pair census, unknown, cutoff or resource failure provides no no-good.
