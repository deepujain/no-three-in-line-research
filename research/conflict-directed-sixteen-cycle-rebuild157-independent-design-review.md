# Independent design review: conflict-directed sixteen-cycle rebuild

Decision: PASS as a restricted construction design, subject to separate implementation, independent source/tiny review and lead allocation. Reviewed `research/conflict-directed-sixteen-cycle-rebuild157-proposal.md` SHA-256 `e08c066ae07885bcda24c7813ff1a0dc706d81bcfc267e4056ba8270a8279777`. No order-79 conflict cover, matching, geometry, candidate, or search was computed in this review.

Given the previously audited input A with exactly five conflict triples, a selected set F that intersects every triple leaves no forbidden triple in R=A minus F: every triple remaining in R would also have been an original triple disjoint from F. F has 16 distinct cells in distinct rows and columns, so R has 141 cells. Any completed replacement M using the same rows and columns once preserves every row and column degree, including the single deficient row and column. The one-cycle permutation requires no fixed point and produces one 32-edge alternating cycle; forbidding every cell of S additionally guarantees that old and new edges are distinct. Validity of the R union M endpoint follows from checking each insertion against the exact secants of all already selected points, assuming correct primitive-line enumeration and rollback.

Since A=S minus one cell, R retains exactly 141 of S. All new cells are outside S, so any full endpoint has 16 additions and exactly 17 effective deletions from S. A 15-insertion prefix has 156 cells, 15 additions and the same 17 deletions. These counts are independent of any particular geometry and place successful endpoints outside the fixed-source deletion caps through 8 and the 9–12 intrinsic SAT range; the 9–12 run timed out, so it is not an exclusion. This distance statement is specific to S and does not establish distance from other seeds or unrelated closed families. A diagnostic prefix is not an improved lower bound.

The first-unhit-conflict tree can branch at most three ways per level and has depth at most five: the selected cell hits the currently unhit triple. The raw upper bound 1+3+9+27+81+243=364 nodes and at most 243 leaves holds, including branches stopped early. For any matching F hitting all original triples, following a cell of F on each unhit triple traces a branch to a matching kernel contained in F. Hence no matching kernel excludes a 16-cell matching cover of these specific five triples, not a general construction. For j selected matching cells, each selected row and column together cover at most three original A cells, so at most 3j of the 157 cells are blocked; for j<16, a legal next old cell necessarily exists. This proves greedy padding reaches 16, but does not show the selected greedy padding or first eight deduplicated F sets are representative or feasible. Failure of those eight is not failure of every padding, kernel extension or 16-cycle.

A partial injective label map has in-degree and out-degree at most one. If adding i->j closes a proper directed cycle, its vertices already have both incident arcs assigned, so later assignments cannot join those vertices to the remaining labels. Pruning that closure preserves every single 16-cycle. A closing arc forming the full 16-cycle on the final insertion must be accepted; an explicit full-cycle validation remains necessary. In tiny exhaustive tests, check arcs that close a cycle of each proper length, including a fixed point, and a legal final long-cycle closure. The root allowed-cell matching is necessary only: a perfect matching can be geometrically incompatible in combination or split into multiple cycles. A root nonmatching conclusion requires complete domains plus an independently checked maximum-matching/vertex-cover certificate.

The stated conservative visit bounds are arithmetically correct: 9870*79*8=6237840 root visits; 156*79*1000=12324000 insertion visits; sum 18561840, plus separately counted matching, validation and bookkeeping. A line iterator must count each lattice cell at most once for the factor 79 to apply. A deadline interrupt during root-domain building, matching, certificate validation or an insertion must never mark that patch closed. A cap is shared across patches and counts starts, including interrupted insertions; a full exhausting search of an F is distinct from a timeout or cap. These are bounds on operations and live representations, not demonstrated runtime or RSS.

The large direct exchange is materially different from the already audited local walk and one/two-move censuses: it rebuilds a conflict-covering matching as one 16-cycle and does not require the intermediate full-cardinality states of any small-switch factorization to be valid or stay below a score barrier. It also differs from the whole-matching reconstruction's retained anchor and fixed target domain. This difference does not predict success or warrant repetition. A saved 157-point result must pass both independent validators, direct source-distance and exact cycle/axis checks; a completed second validator followed by an expired soft deadline must still lead immediately to atomic persistence. No n79 actual allocation follows from this review.
