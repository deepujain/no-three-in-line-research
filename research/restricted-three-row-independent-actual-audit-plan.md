This plan covers a future separately reserved Root-owned audit of one fixed65 restricted-three-row allocation. It executes no loader, actual geometry or optimizer and allocates no retry. Source is currently moving from preserved v2 to an ownership-only v3; implementation and final source/receipt pins must bind the eventual frozen version rather than assume v2. No directory discovery, alternate coordinates, checkpoint resume or orphan adoption is permitted. Authenticate raw bytes before interpreting them, apply byte/count/schema limits and recheck hashes after auditing. Normalize the manifest's paths, reject aliases/self entry, then add exactly its own authenticated digest. Report pins and receipt `sources_before` must equal this effective mapping. Report both the original manifest count and effective count=original+1; only impose a numerical original count if it is separately supplied by Root for the actual freeze. Never reuse a prior worker's count. Require exact final worker, unchanged kernel/shared-helper, verifier, runtime and fixed handoff pins, then stream-check every pin before and after.

The owned receipt must independently bind exact argv, manifest digest, source-unchanged observation, successful process exit, clean cleanup, absent monitor/trigger errors, finite ordered worker/guard/job times within the authorized limits, valid positive sample counts and the authorized sampled-RSS bound. Recorded sampled RSS remains an observation, not a proved unsampled maximum. The audit's own guard/output reread remains a separate final gate.

## Pure helper: direct accepted states and ledger fold

Start from authenticated fixed65 points with zero new started/completed/accepted counters, generation0 and cursor0. Direct determinant enumeration verifies149 distinct grid cells, exactly one singleton row, U65 and the fixed normalized identity. For every distinct accepted state, directly recount triples and derive I/J/K by literal row incidence, M by conflict-union membership, and the K>0 order sorted by(-M,r,s,t). Verify the exact current-state/order identities independently; do not reconstruct them by invoking a production optimizer or loader.

Fold at most16 attempt records in sequence. Each start must select the current candidate at the current cursor, bind current state/order/score and increment started without refund. Capture a `last_start_charge` immediately after that increment, before interpreting the record's outcome. A pending record must be final, remains charged and leaves its cursor uncovered. A complete nonimproving record increments completed and advances one current cursor. An accepted record increments completed/accepted, binds its immutable artifact, strictly lowers direct score, increments generation, installs its points and resets old candidate/cursor identities. Counts never reset across acceptance.

The last-start charge is not the final completed counters. It must be derived from this fold, including the accepted count before the last outcome. Compare it exactly with `report.last_attempted_charge` when any start exists; when no start exists, require no charge field. This derives the value from the saved ledger rather than guessing95 versus96 or weakening the check to an inequality.

Every accepted move must preserve all unselected points, replace exactly the recorded three distinct rows, preserve total selected load5 or6 and the global singleton pattern, match its declared X/Y/Z columns and remain within its recorded four-column domains. Check all domains are sorted, distinct, in-grid, sized4 and include each prior row's current columns. Match move before/after identities, attempt ordinal, generation, exact post-result counters, old cursor/order, optimum-established flag and positive-enumeration-complete requirement to the attempt and reconstructed chain. The direct after-state score must equal the result score and be strictly lower.

The audit does not rerun unary ranking/domain selection or any restricted minimization. Recorded domain choice and nonimproving minima remain source-supported execution. Their schemas, current inclusion, support/load restrictions and ledger associations are audited; no independent minimum/exclusion certificate is claimed. This keeps the endpoint/chain audit separate from optimization replay. If independent exact domain selection is later requested, it requires a separately bounded component-audit design.

## Authority, side artifacts and report

The final authenticated authority must match the reconstructed points/U/identity/generation/counters/pending/cursor. Materialized orders must equal direct rankings. Absent orders are valid only after reset with no residual current-state coverage; the16th accepted move may stop at the cap before constructing a new list. A durable final-stop status is copied from run, so do not require internal `active` status after cap/deadline. Bind each claimed terminal condition: zero to direct zero; block_cap to16 starts; candidate exhaustion to the end of the exact current candidate list; deadline/error to an incomplete or otherwise truthful durable boundary. Receipt acceptance is checked separately from chain consistency.

Verify report state/attempts/artifacts/order hash against the same last durable authority and its exact byte digest/length. For a clean unambiguous successful run, publication count is initialization+one start and one result per completed attempt+one start for a pending attempt+final stop; check against the actual source's publication rules and≤34 rather than guessing a fixed outcome count. For failed/uncertain publication, do not infer successful adoption from an incremented publication counter.

An accepted zero must have a durable witness reference matching its points and an independent direct zero recount. A separately saved zero witness can coexist with old positive/pending authority after later publication failure. A raw candidate or positive orphan is independently classified but never adopted into the chain. Explicit side artifacts are authenticated by the supplied references and may consume the one pending attempt's state slot. Preserve witnesses even if final pin/deadline/report/receipt acceptance fails. Saved geometry, durable authority and overall acceptance are separate output fields.

If there is a pending unadopted result, at most15 earlier accepts are possible, leaving a seventeenth distinct-state slot for its explicitly supplied candidate/witness/orphan; identical side states are cached by identity after point equality validation. I/J/K/M/order derivation reuses those literal conflicts; no extra optimizer or old-history replay occurs.

Use a25-second logical soft bound within a separate≤30-second/256MiB owned audit allocation, subject to Root approval. Check time during streamed file reads and bounded triple loops. Authority≤1MiB, move/witness≤16KiB each, at most16 references/attempts, bounded manifest metadata and streamed source hashing keep memory explicit. These are design ceilings, not a runtime guarantee. Stop and retain a failed audit if time/pins/schema fail; no automatic retry or cap expansion.
