## Exact implemented scope

The fixed adapter is **successful-run only**. It authenticates the five Root-specified documents and loads only move/witness artifacts explicitly referenced by that authority, with basename and byte caps. It rejects unsuccessful or uncertain reports. It does not discover, load, or classify actual failure-side raw candidates, unreferenced saved witnesses, or orphan moves. The pure helper accepts explicitly supplied side outputs and its saved-zero/old-positive-pending and positive-orphan scenarios are synthetic capability tests only; they are not coverage of actual failure artifacts by this adapter.

The pure helper directly checks accepted-state point legality, determinant scores, state identities, conflict incidence, ranked candidate triples, ledger/cursor/counter bindings, selected-domain membership and immutable move associations. Changed-row support is contained in the selected triple; a selected row may remain unchanged. No requirement that all three selected rows change is imposed. A successful decrease from a selected triple does not establish that three changed rows were necessary or that a pair-local minimum was escaped.

Recorded optimizer minima and lowest-unary domain selection remain **source-supported**, not independently recomputed. Domain schema, inclusion of old columns, selected support/load, and direct accepted scores are checked. The adapter performs no optimizer replay or old-history replay and does not claim an exclusion certificate, a pair-stuck certificate, or a valid149-point construction from a positive-score state.

Each distinct accepted/explicit side state is counted once in the helper's identity cache, with equality checks for repeated identities. The initial state is recounted once. The adapter compares the initial-order hash with the helper-verified first attempt order, or the final materialized order when there was no start; it does not recount initial geometry again. The helper enforces at most17 distinct states and16 attempts. The proposed future bound remains at most17*C(149,3)=9,184,658 determinant triple tests, 25 logical seconds within a separately owned30-second/256MiB audit allocation. These are ceilings, not measured actual performance.

The fixed adapter separately requires the externally supplied228 original entries and derives229 effective pins. Source/input/runtime hashes are checked before and after. The last-start charge is derived during the helper's ledger fold immediately after incrementing started and before the attempt outcome; an accepted sixteenth result therefore permits charge16/15/0 and final counters16/16/1. The output writer uses file fsync and atomic replace. No parent-directory fsync or stronger crash-durability claim is made. Sampled RSS is an observation, not an unsampled maximum. A failure is preserved and does not authorize retry.
