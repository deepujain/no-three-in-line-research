## Bounded schema and ownership

A prospective new suffix worker has one authoritative checkpoint containing a complete internally consistent tuple: current points/normalized identity/raw score, accepted ordinal, active phase, current-state ordered priority identities and completed-prefix cursors, sweep flags, cumulative started/completed counters, current pending attempt if any, bounded new-attempt ledger and immutable artifact references. An outer payload digest and owned receipt bind its bytes. Every cursor is tied to state identity, ranking-policy identity and full ordered-list identity. Row and pair totals never reset on acceptance. A generation is the cumulative accepted ordinal, initially28; it is not a substitute for the normalized point identity.

There are at most268 new attempt records, comprising at most204 rows and64 pairs across all subsequent states. Each record starts pending and may become completed or explicitly incomplete/abandoned; an incomplete start remains charged. The old32 pair decisions are represented by one source-bound prefix assertion and immutable provenance references, not32 invented minimum records. If an event-file representation is chosen instead, at most536 immutable start/result records may describe those268 attempts; it must retain the same single authoritative checkpoint rule. Do not retain an unbounded history or all candidate assignments.

For the simpler inline-ledger representation, cap each new attempt record at1024 compact-JSON bytes, each current ordered pair entry at64 bytes, each row-order entry at16 bytes, accepted-artifact references at512 bytes each, point encoding at4096 bytes and remaining fixed/header/provenance metadata at65536 bytes. At most268 records,2775 current pair entries,75 row entries and65 new accepted-artifact references fit below1MiB in aggregate; enforce the1MiB full checkpoint cap in addition to component caps. Current ordered lists replace earlier generations' lists; earlier records retain their state/order hashes. New full-coordinate improvement artifacts are at most65 files of16KiB each. The in-memory envelope still holds current/pending complete geometry only, one current ranking and one serialized checkpoint buffer/pending buffer; it does not cache every accepted state's index.

## Conceptual interface, without implementation

The following names describe reviewable responsibilities, not implemented APIs or a general resume CLI:

- `authenticate_fixed_suffix_handoff(manifest, metadata, check) -> Authority`: authenticate the accepted original run/audit chain and exact current65 state, verify cumulative counters308/32, accepted28 and no pending old optimizer, then perform only newly charged input recount/index/ranking construction. Materialize current rankings and initial pair cursor32. Imported coverage is explicitly source-supported. Reject a different status/state/order policy or incomplete prior pair.
- `plan_start(authority) -> PendingAuthority`: select exactly the next current-state row/pair by phase and completed cursor; reject exhausted cumulative caps; increment its started total; append one bounded pending record with attempt sequence, state/order hash and selected cursor/row labels. This is a pure transition. The runner atomically persists the entire returned authority before calling any optimizer.
- `evaluate_row/pair(kernel_state, pending, check) -> ExactResult`: call the unchanged reviewed pure exact kernel with current included, strict raw policy, global lexical tie rule and first-zero behavior. The kernel receives no active weights/RNG and cannot mutate authoritative state or cursors. A result distinguishes exact optimum established from candidate enumeration exhausted.
- `plan_nonimprovement(pending_authority, result) -> CompletedAuthority`: require a complete exact nonimprovement, whose minimum must equal current U by current eligibility; increment completed, mark that attempt complete, advance only the relevant completed prefix and update sweep/phase when that exact ordered list is exhausted. Keep the same points/state identity. Result columns/minimum support audit but are not an independent optimum certificate.
- `prepare_positive_accept(pending_authority, result, rebuilt_state) -> (MoveArtifact, NextAuthority)`: require strictly lower positive U, full rebuilt exact score, cardinality/row invariants and unchanged unselected rows. Construct the immutable move artifact with old/new identities, old coverage, columns and counters. The proposed next authority references it, increments completed/accepted, installs new points/state, clears both sweep flags, both completed prefixes and stale order identities, and sets phase `row`. No global counter resets. Computing this plan is not commitment.
- `publish_nonimprovement/accept(plan) -> DurableAuthority`: perform the atomic protocol below. No next optimization or external accepted-state notification starts before this succeeds. The runner adopts the new in-memory authority only after the authoritative replacement succeeds; the old complete authority remains active while preparing the plan.
- `handle_exact_zero(pending_authority, result, check) -> SavedZeroOrRaw`: apply the special validation/save sequence below before optional ledger/result/report writes. It returns a preserved candidate/witness identity separately from overall receipt acceptance.
- `inspect_recovery(authoritative_bytes, referenced_artifacts, expected_pins) -> ValidatedAuthorityOrError`: a testable parser/consistency boundary, not authorization to resume a crashed actual run. Validate exactly the one authoritative snapshot and every referenced immutable file. A pending record remains charged and uncovered; an orphan file cannot advance a cursor or accepted generation. Any actual subsequent recovery/allocation still requires Root's explicit review and handoff.

An attempt sequence ranges over the at most268 new attempts, separately from inherited cumulative totals; do not invent a historical ordering for the340 prior starts. A pending attempt has no completed coverage merely because the optimizer may have finished before a crash. A later explicitly authorized retry may repeat that unresolved neighborhood, consuming another start; completed-prefix neighborhoods are skipped without replay. Such retries can cause a cap stop earlier than an uninterrupted trace, which must be reflected honestly.

## Single durable commit protocol

For an ordinary start or a completed nonimprovement, serialize and validate the entire next authoritative tuple and atomically replace the checkpoint using bounded temporary-file write, file fsync, replace and parent-directory fsync. A start that cannot persist never calls the optimizer. Conservatively retain its charge in an error boundary when possible, and fail the receipt. Do not continue after a failed ledger publication.

For a positive acceptance, fully prepare and verify the new state in scratch space. First write/fsync the immutable coordinate move artifact under a unique attempt/generation identity. Then atomically replace the single authoritative checkpoint with new points, both reset cursors/flags, counters and its artifact hash/reference together. Only after that replacement succeeds may the runner adopt the new active state, notify an observer or start Stage1. The artifact is written before its reference becomes authoritative. This supersedes any reading of the original memo that would separately persist new points and old cursors, or mutate the committed in-memory state before the durable checkpoint.

A failure before authoritative replacement leaves the old authoritative state plus the already charged pending start; a newly written move file is an orphan, not an accepted state. A crash after replacement but before callback/in-memory adoption recovers the new complete authority. A file/directory-fsync or replace failure with uncertain durability fails overall acceptance and stops work; preserve both candidate evidence and existing files. A later explicitly reviewed recovery validates the one checkpoint actually present as a whole rather than assembling a hybrid from filenames, old counters and orphan artifacts. Never adopt an orphan merely because its score is lower. No automatic retry, fallback source or cap reset follows an error.

Two authoritative writes per attempted optimization suffice: start and completed result/acceptance. With at most268 attempts, initialization and final-stop publication, the design ceiling is538 checkpoint publications, each at most1MiB, plus bounded immutable artifacts/reports. That is a finite I/O envelope, not an assertion that fsync/cursor overhead fits25 seconds. The new owned synthetic lifecycle/resource gate must measure this added persistence path; historical4.37-second search timing does not include it. Keep the module-entry25soft interval,23search/2shutdown reserve and30owned/256MiB guard. Check before each start/publication and inside every kernel loop; a slow syscall can still cause a late failed receipt.

## Exact zero priority

The start ledger is already durable and charged when evaluation finds a fully scored lexical-first zero. Do not first publish its result record, reindex it, write a raw checkpoint, materialize a new order, notify an observer or write a move artifact. Validate row/cardinality structure without optional geometry, start validator one timely, start validator two only if still timely, and save the witness immediately after successful validator two even if it finishes late. No clock/pin/ledger work intervenes between that successful second validator and witness save.

After witness persistence, the worker may publish a zero authoritative state referencing the witness, with completed/accepted increments and reset coverage, then final artifacts/pins/report. If the ledger publication fails, the witness remains saved while the overall receipt fails; the old authoritative state and its charged pending record remain distinct from that independently auditable witness file. If validation/save is incomplete or disagrees, preserve the raw zero candidate after the validator attempt, stop with the prior committed positive authority and charged unresolved attempt, and label candidate validation separately. Do not silently commit an unvalidated candidate as an accepted witness. The independent witness auditor may later assess either preserved output under its own allocation. A later source/deadline/report failure never deletes a saved witness.

This intentionally distinguishes last durable active state, exact kernel zero candidate, dual-validated saved witness and accepted overall receipt. The pure minimum calculation may establish a zero before every candidate is enumerated; no prefix-coverage or complete-enumeration claim is required to preserve it.

## Three-row sketch scope

The separate restricted three-row alternative remains a structural sketch, not an accepted controller or fallback. Its16-block ceiling means16 total started block optimizations across the entire hypothetical allocation, including interrupted blocks and every accepted-state reset; it is never16 per state. A block neighborhood is keyed by state identity plus its three rows and restricted-domain identity. Acceptance invalidates old-state selection/coverage, but cannot refund starts or allocate another16 blocks. Any actual selection/restart/row-followup policy and its cumulative costs require their own design review; this addendum authorizes none.
