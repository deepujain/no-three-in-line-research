# Lead review of the conditional n75 contraction design

Reviewed design SHA-256 `ff2faa8667fbe21b69e473068f5fa692ca8cc88194c781b3e4194c53ffdd5632` on 27 November 2026, independently of its author. This is a mathematical design review, not a source review or search allocation.

The cardinality and axis argument is sound. A full 2N configuration has exactly two points on each axis level. An occupied row/column intersection therefore removes three distinct points. Removing those coordinate levels is injective on survivors, preserves their remaining axis loads, and produces exactly 2N−3 points. This argument does not preserve oblique noncollinearity.

The rectangle counting argument is also sound. Between consecutive distinct coordinates of a source triple, every contraction indicator is constant. Cuts equal to a triple coordinate remove that triple and must be excluded. The resulting disjoint rectangles partition precisely the cuts where all three points survive. Their compressed determinant is constant, so adding one to every zero-determinant rectangle counts each surviving forbidden triple exactly once. Injectivity ensures that image triples have unique source triples. A two-dimensional difference array and prefix sum implement this count without storing triples. The stated 16 tests per unordered source triple and compact array bounds are conservative; they are not timing guarantees.

The author's all-small-axis-full test comparison is useful evidence and remains labeled as authored validation, despite the helper filename containing “independent.” A future implementation still requires separate source/tiny and resource review. Exact interval endpoints, omission of removed triples, signed difference updates, completion counters, and both validators on any proposed witness are material requirements.

Decision: retain the n76→75 proposal conditionally. The focused literature report strongly overlaps the n78→77 family with the earlier 6,084-cut compression experiment; do not allocate that census. Before implementing n75, authenticate the selected 152-point n76 witness, check attributable n75/149 results and prior contraction coverage, and settle whether a useful target remains. No contraction at n75 or n77, witness import, or numerical allocation was performed in this review. The fixed original deadline and aggregate budget remain unchanged.
