Direct determinants enumerate every triple and sum its line weight. A separate Fraction-based line normal checks keys. It uses ordinary coordinate tuples and member lists, not production bit masks.

Preparation took4.733255 seconds; this is synthetic oracle timing, not production or n75 performance.
