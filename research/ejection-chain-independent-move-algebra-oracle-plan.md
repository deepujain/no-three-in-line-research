## General exact toggling identities

Let A be a set of 2n-1 distinct cells with row/column degrees at most 2. Let F be distinct removed cells contained in A, and E be distinct inserted cells disjoint from A. Require |F|=|E|=k. Then A'=(A minus F) union E has the same cardinality and no duplicate cells. For each row or column vertex v, its degree change is exactly incident_E(v) minus incident_F(v). This equation, not a move name, is the independent axis oracle. Degree upper bound 2 plus cardinality 2n-1 implies one degree-1 vertex and all other vertices degree 2 on each side. Explicitly check the endpoint degrees rather than inferring them from a partial construction.

For fixed source S, define D(A)=|S minus A| and N(A)=|A minus S|. Then

    D(A') = D(A) + |F intersect S| - |E intersect S|
    N(A') = N(A) - |F minus S| + |E minus S|.

These formulas hold for any legal disjoint removal/insertion sets, including source readdition in a generic move API. If |S|=2n and |A|=2n-1, D(A)=N(A)+1. The raw number k removed from the current state is not generally the effective source deletion count. Report and test both; compute final counts directly as an independent check.

## Current proposed 16-cycle patch

Engineer describes A=S minus one cell and a matching F={(r_i,c_i): i=0..15}, with distinct rows and distinct columns, hitting every one of A's independently established five conflict triples. Deleting F therefore leaves a geometrically valid 141-point set R: every triple of R was already a triple of A, and none of A's conflicting triples survives intact. This implication requires an exhaustive, independently verified conflict list, not merely five selected examples.

Insert M={(r_i,c_sigma(i))}, where sigma is a bijection of the 16 selected indices. One removal and one insertion occur on every selected row and column, so all axis loads and both deficiency locations are unchanged, even if a deficient axis is selected. Requiring every new cell outside S makes M disjoint from all of A. It follows that the final set has 157 points, retains exactly 141 source points, inserts exactly 16 non-source points and has exactly D=17 source deletions. The change lies beyond earlier source-retention scopes through D=12; no inference about feasibility follows.

Requiring sigma to be one 16-cycle makes the edge symmetric difference one alternating cycle of length 32 in the row/column graph. A rectangle is only a 2-cycle; a union of small disjoint cycles does not satisfy this condition. The final long cycle may have an algebraic decomposition into small switches, but the proposed constructive search does not require those intermediate states to be geometrically valid or accepted by a Metropolis rule.

A partial sigma is an injective partial map, represented by directed arcs i to sigma(i). Its components are directed paths, isolated vertices, or closed directed cycles. An arc that closes a proper cycle is incompatible with a final single 16-cycle: all vertices in the closed cycle already have their unique incoming/outgoing arcs and cannot later join another component. Reject exactly such a proper cycle. Closing the cycle on all 16 vertices is the required success condition and must not be pruned. A 1-cycle is excluded by the outside-S requirement as well as by proper-cycle pruning. Tiny tests must inspect cycle length, not simply reject every encounter with an already visited vertex.

Selecting a first uncovered conflict and branching over its three points has depth at most five, provided already covered conflicts are skipped and every selected point is new. Reject row/column collisions in the deletion kernel. At most 3^5 leaves are possible; a full naive recursion tree has at most 364 nodes, not 243. A feasible matching kernel always admits greedy padding to 16 in this degree-two 157-edge graph: each chosen edge excludes itself and at most one other edge at each endpoint, so k chosen edges exclude at most 3k original edges. For k<=15, at least 157-45 compatible edges remain. This proves padding exists, not that a conflict-hitting matching kernel exists, nor that one greedy padding represents all useful paddings. Taking the first eight distinct padded patches is deliberately incomplete.

## Open deficiency-path algebra, separate from current proposal

In the row/column incidence graph, take a vertex-simple path of even length 2k whose edges alternate absent/present, beginning with an insertion edge and ending with a deletion edge. Toggle the path. There are k inserted and k removed cells; internal vertices have one insertion and one deletion and preserve degree. The endpoints lie on the same bipartition side. The initial endpoint gains one degree and the final endpoint loses one degree. Therefore a legal deficiency migration starts at the unique deficient row and ends at a different full row, or starts at the unique deficient column and ends at a different full column. The deficiency moves to the final endpoint; degrees on the other side stay unchanged. The reverse path, with insertion/removal roles interchanged in the new state, is legal. k=1 is the existing relocation; k>=2 gives a longer compound path.

An odd alternating path changes cardinality by one and cannot alone be a 2n-1 move. Repeated vertices, occupied insertion cells, or a deficient deletion endpoint require separate treatment and should be rejected by a simple-path API. A chain assembled through temporary vacancies need only have the promised axis pattern at its completed endpoint; do not claim that deleting all old cells first preserves the full axis pattern at intermediate steps. The current 16-cycle design preserves deficiency locations and makes no open-path coverage claim.

## Exact score telescope and validity

Let C(P) count unordered collinear triples and let I(p,Q) count pairs of distinct cells of Q collinear with p, for p not in Q. For a deletion sequence f_1,...,f_k, subtract I(f_j,P minus {f_j}) immediately before each deletion from the current P. Then for insertion sequence e_1,...,e_k, add I(e_j,P) immediately before insertion into the current P. The sum is exactly C(A')-C(A), independent of the chosen legal deletion/insertion ordering. A triple containing several removed cells is subtracted when its first cell is deleted; a triple containing several inserted cells is added when its last cell is inserted. Four points on a line yield four triples: deleting two removes 3 then 1, not 3 plus 3. Primitive-direction bucket pairs implement I exactly; a direct determinant triple oracle must be independent of that implementation.

The current proposed patch deletes a conflict-hitting set and builds only safe insertions. Therefore R is valid, each insertion has I=0 and every constructed partial is valid. It need not maintain a Metropolis energy or score invalid intermediate states. The score telescope remains a useful independent generic move test and explains why the final endpoint, if found, has zero conflicts. Do not introduce energy-search machinery solely to test this identity.
