# From-scratch row-block breakout construction for 149 on 75

Status: DESIGN ONLY. No actual source coordinates were read, no n75/n79 geometry was evaluated, and no numerical search was performed. This proposes a new constructive trajectory, not a further embedding exclusion. Independent engineering, prior-scope, source/tiny and lifecycle review must precede an explicitly allocated actual gate. It does not assert effectiveness or novelty.

## Structural change and exact neighborhood

Use n=75 and k=149. Start without a published source: local `random.Random(275149)` chooses the deficient row uniformly, then chooses one distinct cell there and two distinct cells independently in every other row, in increasing row order. Coordinates are (x,y), with y denoting the row. Keep exactly k distinct points, one row of load one and all others of load two. Impose no symmetry. Column capacity and every other nonhorizontal collinearity are soft search constraints; a final witness must satisfy them exactly.

This representation includes every valid 149-point configuration on this grid: row capacity two and total149 force precisely that row pattern. Its search trajectory does not enumerate the representation. The allowed moves are whole-row replacement and deficiency migration. Whole-row replacement chooses a different unordered pair of cells in a full row, or a different singleton in the deficient row. Deficiency migration removes any point from a full row and inserts a new cell in the currently deficient row, moving the deficiency to the donor row. These moves connect the represented state space: migrate the deficiency to its target row if necessary, then replace each row's cells by its target cells. Connectivity supplies no mixing-time or success guarantee.

The critical computational fact is exact separability. Remove all points in row r, leaving R. For x=(a,r), define its insertion cost as the sum, over lines through x, of w(L)*C(|R intersect L|,2). A candidate pair x,y in row r has total incremental cost cost(x)+cost(y): a triple containing x,y and a point of R would be horizontal, but R has no point in row r. There are at most two new points, so no all-new triple exists. Thus optimize an entire row using n unary costs, with no neglected future mixed interaction and no matching constraint.

Use canonical integer line keys (a,b,c) for ax+by=c, with gcd(|a|,|b|)=1 and first nonzero among a,b positive. At each accepted state build a complete pair-line index, storing the point-membership bit mask for every line containing at least two current points. It has at most C(k,2)=11026 entries. For a selected row, mask out that row's points. For each indexed line retaining t>=2 points, intersect it with y=r: if a!=0 and (c-br) is divisible by a and the resulting x lies in [0,74], add w(L)*C(t,2) to that cell's cost. Horizontal lines cannot intersect this now-empty row. This computes every unary cost exactly using the existing index, without scanning hypothetical added-point pairs or allocating all grid lines.

Enumerate at most C(75,2)=2775 unordered pairs (or75 singletons) to find the minimum cost replacement other than the current row set. Resolve ties using one uniform RNG choice among the lexicographically ordered minimum-cost alternatives. All candidates are in the emptied row, so duplicates with retained points are impossible. Selecting a different row set is mandatory, even if every different replacement increases the weighted score. This is a deliberate escape policy, not descent or a complete local-minimum test.

## One fixed adaptive policy

The unweighted score is U(S)=sum_L C(|S intersect L|,3). It counts all collinear triples, including all C(t,3) triples on a line with t>3. The weighted score uses positive integer weights, initially one, and is zero exactly when U=0. Keep current and best unweighted states separately; never replace the best with a worse one. No source-distance objective or seed-repair restriction is used.

Before each transition, build the complete current pair-line index and score. If U=0, run both independent full validators and persist the witness immediately after both accept, before optional reporting or final pin checks. A failed or incomplete validation is not a witness. Record coordinates, hashes and exact axis counts. No continuation follows a witness.

For transitions whose one-based number is not divisible by16, choose uniformly from the sorted rows containing at least one point on a currently violated line, then perform the exact best-distinct row replacement just defined. All violated lines are nonhorizontal because row load is at most two. In particular the eligible row list is nonempty whenever U>0. No weighted or sampled approximation is used to rank alternatives in this selected row.

Every16th transition instead samples32 legal deficiency migrations with replacement: choose a donor uniformly from the sorted points outside the deficient row, and a destination uniformly from the sorted vacant cells in the deficient row. Duplicate resulting states count toward32 attempts but are considered once. Calculate each exact weighted delta by sequential removal then insertion, or an independently equivalent line-count calculation; the two-row move must not use the same-row separability lemma. Choose uniformly among the sorted distinct minimum-score sampled outcomes, and accept it even if uphill. Every sample is legal, so there is at least one outcome when the complete sample finishes. Do not choose from an interrupted sample.

After each accepted transition, rebuild the complete index before assessing U and updating best. If U strictly improves the globally best unweighted score, reset the stagnation counter. Otherwise increment it. At32 consecutive transitions without a strict global-best improvement, increase by one the weight of every line currently containing at least three points, and reset the stagnation counter. Recompute the weighted score under these new weights before the next decision. These persistent penalties change the objective; the best unweighted score remains the meaningful diagnostic. Never claim a weighted-score decrease is a geometrical improvement. There is no tabu list, restart, alternate seed, source import, cooling schedule, population, repair-cap expansion or automatic switch to n79.

Store nondefault weights for at most65536 distinct line keys. Weights may not exceed65535. Before a penalty update, check that the entire update fits both bounds; if not, stop with the named cap and leave the last completed state intact. Do not partially apply an update or drop old weights. A step cap of200000 also bounds penalty events by6250, so the weight-value cap is conservative; the distinct-key cap can still bind.

## Finite cost and resource gate

One state has149 points. A full index reconstruction evaluates11026 pairs, with small exact integer arithmetic and gcd normalization. One selected-row cost pass scans at most11026 lines and2775 pairs. A migration step has32 attempts; sequential removal/insertion can be evaluated with at most2*k point-line incidence operations per attempt, plus the complete index reconstruction needed for the next state. Reuse the next-state index at the following step: do not rebuild it twice. With T<=200000 transitions there are at most T+1 full state indexes, hence at most2205211026 pair constructions. This is a finite work ceiling, not an 80-minute runtime prediction.

With normalized keys, |a|,|b|<=74 and |c|<=10952; point masks use149 bits. Keep only current/next index as needed,65536 weights, current/best149-point states, one75-cell cost vector, at most2775 tie candidates or a bounded tie-selection equivalent,32 migration records, and bounded scalar telemetry. No line table over all possible grid lines and no unbounded history are needed. The operation/key bounds make a256-MiB implementation plausible, but Python object/allocator and runtime overhead must be measured by the engineer's synthetic worst-envelope gate; this document does not claim a proved RSS bound. Any gate remains under the unchanged owned sampled256-MiB guard.

The first actual proposal is one fresh25-second soft /30-second owned pilot, one seed, with the same200000 transition ceiling (the time limit will likely bind). Count full and partial transitions, row/migration steps, pair-index work, penalty events, weight-key maximum, exact initial/best/final unweighted scores, bounded checkpoints at powers of two, and cleanup. Retain only initial/current/best state artifacts and at most64 telemetry checkpoints; cap report output at1MiB. Dual-validate the saved best diagnostic at shutdown if time permits, distinguishing positive-score invalid states from valid witnesses. Preserve a completed zero witness even if the second validator finishes beyond the soft deadline. If the first finishes after the deadline, do not initiate the second. Final pins and receipt acceptance remain required for declaring the run accepted.

The first pilot is a measured construction attempt, not a promise of149. Do not preauthorize80minutes. A sustained invocation of at most3600 soft seconds (with an independently reviewed owned allowance and within Root's aggregate budget) is reasonable to consider only after an accepted pilot shows useful completed moves within resource limits, genuine strict reductions in unweighted score after the initial descent, successful deficiency migrations, and distinct accepted states across penalty events. These diagnostics motivate consideration, not evidence of proximity to a witness. If the pilot simply cycles, fails correctness/resources, never completes migration, or only changes weighted scores, curtail it. Any continuation needs an explicit Root decision and a reviewed exact state/weights/RNG continuation contract; it is not an automatic retry or a new seed. No continuation implementation is included in the first pilot scope.

## Prior overlap and falsifiers

The prior uniform Metropolis and focused-tabu157 walks started from the invalid score-five source and preserved both row and column capacities. This method deliberately permits overloaded columns and reconstructs an entire row exactly, with persistent adaptive line weights and no published source. The distinction is not merely a new seed or temperature. Earlier paired/packed methods fixed permutation or symmetry structure; matching rebuild required geometric validity during growth or hard residual column matching. Earlier greedy deletion repair retained a valid prefix and rebuilt from an existing source; the present method keeps149 points throughout and tolerates geometric conflicts. The fixed64-cell Boolean scaffold and selected affine/insertion/contraction exclusions do not exhaust this state space. Nevertheless adaptive penalties, breakout and row optimization are established heuristic ideas; no algorithmic or construction novelty is asserted. The previously tried trade search must receive a focused source-level overlap review before implementation approval.

Independent synthetic falsifiers must cover: all small row-valid states and every row replacement at n2..4 against direct determinant scores; same-row additivity including t>=4 lines; a mixed-two-row counterexample rejecting that lemma for migrations; canonical vertical/horizontal/negative-slope keys; exact sequential migration deltas and row-deficiency changes; positive weighted score versus zero equivalence; a forced uphill best-distinct row move; ties and seeded operation-capped reproducibility; weight increments changing the ranking while leaving U unchanged; stale weighted-score rejection after a penalty update; dictionary/value caps without partial updates; complete-versus-interrupted sample behavior; best-state monotonicity; immediate zero-witness persistence; both-validator disagreement; final pin and owned cleanup failures. Tiny state enumeration here is a review requirement, not an assertion that it has run.

Recommendation: retain this as one genuinely distinct constructive pilot, subject to engineering and overlap review. It is preferable to spending another gate on an affine derivative or an enlarged dead repair neighborhood. If review shows an equivalent existing row/soft-column/adaptive-weight policy already ran, or the implementation cannot meet the resource envelope, prune it honestly rather than rename it.
