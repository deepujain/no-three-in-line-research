# Proposed bounded row-batch continuation

Design only. First require independent approval of the exact combined dispatcher and adapter, and passing real-host n=3/n=5 initial/resume gates. Then a separately allocated n=79, `(d,t)=(0,0)`, 100-node cost pilot may proceed under the approved 30-second and 256-MiB sampled guard. Record its full source and row-batch identity, clean helper/cleanup result, exact node count, sampled RSS, elapsed time, witness directory and accepted checkpoint SHA; an early timeout, resource/monitor/cleanup failure, source mismatch, rejected or missing checkpoint, or witness requires inspection and stops this proposal.

If that pilot ends cleanly at `node_limit` with 100 nodes, no witness, stable approved sources and an independently validated accepted frontier, consider one 500-node continuation of the same `(0,0)` slice. Pin the exact accepted pilot digest via `--expected-sha256`, use the same checkpoint with a new report path, and retain the same 30-second and 256-MiB gates. Accept no new frontier without a clean guard, matching report/bytes, predecessor, full row-batch schema/source/orbit/branch identity and checked cumulative counters. The fivefold node cap is deliberately small; pilot timing and sampled RSS do not prove the next 500 nodes fit. Stop and reassess after this one continuation, on timeout or guard failure, any witness, or exhaustion; retain witnesses and independently verify geometry and reflection before reporting.

The row-batch checkpoint has its own versioned identity and traversal. Its frontier cannot resume an old single-orbit frontier, and the old single-orbit chain runner must not be pointed at it: a future row-batch chain needs its own explicitly reviewed schema, guard bindings, accounting, tests and real-host gates. No coverage fraction, unrestricted infeasibility, novelty or speedup claim follows from a bounded pilot or continuation.
