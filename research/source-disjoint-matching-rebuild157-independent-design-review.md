# Independent review: source-disjoint matching rebuild

Decision: PASS for design, conditional on the implementation and independent source/tiny gates. Reviewed `research/source-disjoint-matching-rebuild157-proposal.md` SHA-256 `255821c91fa8f3364bd6d39cd98e20c9e536e577d863775d92ee0a82f091f730`. This review used algebra and source inspection only; it performed no n=79 decomposition, domain construction, scoring, matching or search.

The degree-two simple bipartite graph of B is a disjoint union of alternating even cycles, including components of arbitrary even length. Alternating each cycle gives two perfect matchings. Starting at each component's lexicographically smallest edge and assigning that edge color zero fixes the retained matching independent of which endpoint is traversed first: on an even cycle both traversal directions give the same alternating coloring. The implementation must verify the source has 156 distinct in-range cells and exactly two edges per old row and column, and must verify the result contains one edge at each old axis. R is a subset of the verified valid B, so R is valid.

A perfect matching M of 79 rows to 79 columns contributes 79 distinct cells. M avoids B, hence cannot overlap R; R union M has 157 cells. Old rows and columns have two cells each and the added row and column have one each. M's disjointness from B makes the B deletion count exactly 78. M's disjointness from S means the final intersection with S is at most |R| = 78, and therefore at least 80 cells of the 158-point S are deleted. The guarantee survives arbitrary overlap between B and S. It defines a distinct source-distance family and does not imply feasibility, novelty or search success.

For distinct grid points a and b, the primitive integer direction obtained by dividing their difference by the coordinate gcd enumerates precisely the integer lattice cells on their Euclidean line when extended through the board. The root union of pair secants is thus exactly the set of cells that would form a triple with two points of R. By induction, after adding a safe cell p, adding the lines through p and every previously selected point gives all and only newly relevant pair secants. Independent determinant tiny tests should include opposite orientations, nonprimitive offsets, horizontal/vertical lines and four collinear cells. Bitsets are immutable values; rollback must restore the complete prior bitset, assigned-row mask and used-column mask, including after interruption.

The root bipartite graph omits cells blocked by R's secants or B union S. Any completion induces a perfect matching in that graph, so maximum matching below 79 is a sound necessary-condition obstruction for this fixed R and forbidden set. A size-79 matching alone does not check collinearity among M cells. For a negative root result, validate a primal matching and a matching-size upper bound, for example the vertex cover from alternating reachability, whose size equals the matching cardinality and which covers every enumerated allowed edge. The complete root domain must be available before reporting a Hall/cover obstruction; partial masks or interrupted matching establish no such result.

MRV with deterministic row and column tie order enumerates the same completions as any other assignment order if and only if every currently safe unused-column choice is explored and rollback is exact. Zero-domain pruning is sound. The 2000-started-insertion limit and 25-second deadline make a partial run inconclusive, and the first full witness must stop actual search as specified. An exhaust-all tiny oracle may use a separate test-only callback to compare all solutions rather than changing the actual witness-stop policy.

The arithmetic ceilings follow when each primitive lattice line visits each in-bounds grid cell at most once: 3003*79=237237 root visits; at most 156*79=12324 visits per candidate, or 24648000 for 2000 candidate starts; and at most 79*6241=493039 edge inspections for an augmenting implementation that scans each edge at most once per root augmentation. These are not runtime or memory measurements. Explicitly count visits consistently with the bound: a two-direction line iterator must not count the shared anchor twice. Check deadline during long propagation and matching loops, and make interruption leave closure=false. Limit stack and masks as proposed, and do not infer that the outer sampled RSS guard catches every instantaneous peak.

Save only a fully dual-verified 157-point witness as a new lower bound; a verified 156-point partial is a diagnostic arrangement and does not improve the existing 156-point baseline. Verify source distances directly on saved points, not just by construction. Persist a completed dual-verified witness even if the soft deadline elapses during validator two; a timeout before validator two leaves no claim. Any apparent contradiction of earlier source-local solver results warrants investigation, but this family lies outside those deletion scopes by construction. A completed root cover or exhaustive DFS applies only to the one deterministic retained matching and forbidden cells; it cannot exclude other colorings or unrelated constructions.
