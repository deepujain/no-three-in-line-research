# Bounded valid-state valley walk proposal

Status: design only. The 25-second neutral trial completed 77 passes with deletions 6..12: 76 returned the same 156-point state and one returned fewer points. Its report does not record that smaller trial's size or coordinates. A reasonable hypothesis is that retaining some smaller valid states permits a later repair to leave the rigid seed; the observation does not prove this or show that the smaller trial would meet any chosen floor.

Keep two distinct states. `best` is the largest dual-verified valid certificate seen, initialized to the 156-point seed and never downgraded. `walk` is a separately validated valid state, initially that seed. On every pass delete a seeded uniform integer in 6..12, clipped to the current walk size for small test grids, and scan shuffled vacant cells greedily as before. At a completed pass, independently dual-verify the trial and compute its normalized hash. If it exceeds best, atomically publish the verified improvement immediately, update best, and move walk to it. Otherwise, if its size is between `max(0, best_count - 4)` and `best_count` inclusive and its hash differs from current walk, accept it as the next walk with seeded probability 0.5; reject the rest. The floor follows a newly improved best, so it does not permit unlimited downward drift. The maximum four-point deficit is a bounded exploratory choice, not evidence that four is optimal. Do not accept incomplete-pass neutral or downhill states. Any valid improvement discovered during an incomplete pass must still be dual-verified and published immediately, independently of whether that pass finishes.

For each completed pass record deletion-size histogram and a histogram of resulting trial sizes; record incomplete passes separately. Count candidate evaluations, best improvements, eligible different hashes, accepted and rejected transitions split by size delta (including downward versus equal versus upward from walk), accepted transitions below 156, and the minimum accepted size. Record best/current normalized hashes and the maximum symmetric-difference distance from the original seed among accepted states. Keep a bounded LRU set of accepted hashes with capacity and evictions reported; call counts `new_to_cache`, never lifetime unique after eviction. An unchanged hash is not diversity. A short bounded sample of accepted state hashes and sizes aids audit; retaining coordinates for each accepted state would permit independent validity/distance checks, but must have an explicit small bound and must never grow with time. At least the final walk certificate should be saved separately and dual-verified; the best certificate remains the user-facing incumbent. Source and seed hashes, deterministic RNG seed, time and stop cause must be reported.

Correctness and lifecycle: every input, accepted walk and published best is in range, duplicate-free and valid under both exact verifiers; `best_count` is monotone and the walk floor is enforced against the current best. The original seed is untouched. Publication uses temporary file plus atomic rename with file fsync, without a crash-durability claim for the containing directory. A stopped or failed guarded process may leave a valid already-published improvement, which requires independent verification; report and final walk may be absent. The loop deadline is soft during in-progress checks/verification/write; use the separately approved 30-second/256-MiB owned-process guard around a 25-second probe, with source pins and clean-exit audit. No retries or automatic scale-up.

Focused tiny tests should demonstrate a valid downward transition that stays above the floor; below-floor rejection; a subsequent upward or equal transition from the smaller walk; identical-state rejection; seeded accept/reject decisions; bounded-cache eviction; best never regressing; immediate saved improvement; and incomplete-pass behavior. Check exact deletion and trial-size accounting and valid snapshots with both verifiers. Then one separately reviewed seed 279106, 25-second test can report whether any below-best states are accepted and whether their accepted hashes differ from the seed; same baseline seed and cap aid interpretation but the change in RNG calls and acceptance paths prevents exact paired randomness. A larger uniform destruction range alone may still greedily reconstruct the seed and has no demonstrated advantage; the down-move directly tests the observed rigidity. If no valid distinct below-best state is retained, do not repeat or scale without new evidence. If diversity appears, it is a path-diversity observation, not an improvement or novelty claim; a 157/158 certificate must be independently verified and assessed against known results.
