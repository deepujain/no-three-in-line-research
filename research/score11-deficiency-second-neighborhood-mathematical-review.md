# Three-exit second-neighborhood census: mathematical design review

Decision: PASS for revised proposal `research/score11-deficiency-second-neighborhood-proposal.md` SHA-256 `f153fe7fe7e3990321981458ac0b6c8bf4a6de45b3374604fe601b20b1e2a976`, following acceptance of the independently audited initial census and exact exit manifest. This review used design reasoning and fixed artifact identity reads only. No n=79 neighbor was generated or scored, no second-neighborhood replay was performed and no actual run was launched. A separate source/tiny review and Root allocation remain required.

Accepted input evidence is `data/score11-deficiency-neighborhood-v1-independent-actual-audit.json` SHA-256 `edec993b9493b73d90b879f682fb6624176c75d8796d18f2cce94ea5800f1350` and `data/score11-deficiency-neighborhood-v1-independent-plus1-exit-manifest.json` SHA-256 `717c6c1fb2debc0d446cb67922670c4ad97643b34a54aaaae653e6d1b0f0b71b`. Their mutual references, acceptance/completeness, three exit records, exit-file hashes and local-minimum-certificate hash were checked. The accepted initial audit independently reproduced the full12560-attempt histogram and certified at least six integer collinear triples in every12247 legal neighbor. It identifies exactly three score6 exits from the score5 input: column relocation(77,33) to(77,78), column relocation(77,51) to(77,78), and rectangle {(33,78),(78,0)} to {(33,0),(78,78)}. This review relies on that accepted fixed result; it does not claim to have repeated the initial census.

For each157-point parent, the specified catalog has157 row attempts,157 column attempts and choose(157,2)=12246 unordered rectangle attempts, totaling12560. Three distinct fixed parents therefore give37680 labeled attempts. No other node enters an expansion queue. Exact inverse checks preserve each parent's point set and negate delta, while tiny independent determinant comparisons are still needed because inverse cancellation alone cannot exclude a shared scoring bug. Same-parent duplicate legal neighbors are impossible: a relocation's removed/added cells identify its old cell and changed axis, and a rectangle's two removed cells identify its unique unordered-pair attempt. Cross-parent duplicates and revisits of preseeded states are possible and must be counted without changing the expansion scope.

The occupancy key sum(2**(n*x+y)) is injective for in-range distinct cells at fixed n. The largest possible bit index is6240, so each n=79 key has at most6241 bits. Preseed exactly four distinct keys: source A and the three exits, with exact scores5,6,6,6. At most37680 generated novel keys can then be inserted, so37684 is a valid map-entry bound. Store only bounded metadata per key, not all point arrays or validator reports. Preserve first generating parent and a generated-seen flag separately from preseed membership, so a seeded state reached for the first time is correctly counted in generated uniqueness without becoming novel.

For completed bookkeeping let C be completed attempts, L legal and I illegal: C=L+I. Let U be novel first insertions and D occurrences of a key already present in the preseeded global map: L=U+D and map_size=4+U. Let K be the number of distinct preseeded keys actually generated,0<=K<=4. Then generated_unique=U+K; the novel-score histogram sums to U, and the all-generated-unique score histogram sums to U+K. The histogram over labeled legal outcomes sums to L. Backtracks-to-A and exit hits are preexisting-map occurrences; cross-parent revisits depend on a prior generating parent rather than merely preseed membership. These diagnostic categories may overlap, but the primary conservation partitions above may not. Same-parent repeat count must remain zero. A repeated key's score must agree exactly with its stored score; mismatch is failure. Started and completed attempt counts remain separate on interruption.

The accepted first census gives strict local minimality: every nontrivial first legal move has score>=6, and the three pinned exits exhaust score6. Therefore every strictly improving two-move path starting at A whose maximum score is<=6 must go through one of these three parents. Enumerating all their second moves covers all such paths, as well as second endpoints of larger score that do not satisfy that peak condition. An endpoint score<5 is a strict improvement; score5 is a return or plateau endpoint. Complete enumeration with no score<5 excludes strictly improving two-move paths with peak<=6 under this catalog. It does not exclude the paths themselves, since each exit can backtrack to A, and it does not exclude first moves of score>6, longer paths, different moves, or global existence.

There is a stronger limitation on construction utility. Starting from A, which deletes one point of the fixed158-point source, each allowed move removes at most two currently present cells. Two moves can therefore remove at most four additional original-source cells, and every endpoint has at most five effective source deletions. This entire two-move endpoint family lies inside the previously solver-reported target157 at-most-six-deletion scope, and hence inside the combined at-most-eight scope. The relevant prior independent audits are `51bd1bfdb9f23a1c1f50829d3bcd007e0e742e88d326c99c66fde27f2165785d` (D<=6) and `a4a1f15d8990df04b8cdaf3725e8a1d5ffb7c00637c0b8ec8dab6943ae61de29` (D=7..8); neither contains an independently checked UNSAT proof. Thus this proposed work characterizes invalid-score improvements and the local barrier, rather than opening a new valid-construction region or supporting a claim about search success probability. Any actual zero-score endpoint must still be immediately dual-validated and saved, then investigated against the conditional native negative; it must not be discarded on that negative's authority.

The revised proposal appropriately uses one25-second soft interval from process entry and one owned30-second/sampled256-MiB guard. Input verification, all three parent validations, enumeration, inverse checks and bookkeeping share the deadline. The finite map and compact metadata bound algorithmic growth but do not guarantee process RSS. Complete negative evidence requires all37680 attempts and inverse/bookkeeping operations, plus required best validations, to finish. Timeout, mismatch or hard termination remains incomplete and preserves only earlier verified artifacts. Once both full validators agree on a candidate, save it atomically even if the second validator crosses the soft deadline; a witness stops immediately without claiming census completion. No automatic second gate, recursion, retry, descent or third move is authorized.

Before source freeze, tests must compare complete tiny two-level catalogs and exact scores to an independent determinant oracle, including preseed hits, cross-parent repeats, duplicate-score disagreement, injective keys, all conservation equations, impossible same-parent repeats, deterministic first-parent/tie order, corrupted exits and pins, inverse checks, partial class/parent bookkeeping, validator timeout/disagreement and late witness persistence. All fixed input/audit/manifest/source dependencies must be pinned and rechecked before final publication. No blocking mathematical defect remains in the revised design.
