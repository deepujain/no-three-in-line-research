# Independent suffix authority audit protocol

This is SOURCE/TINY preparation for a separately allocated Root-owned audit. It authorizes no actual input read, actual geometry, optimizer replay, continuation or search. The pure helper accepts explicit bytes and references; it does not discover a directory, import a production worker, invoke a loader or recover a pending attempt. The actual adapter has been source-parsed only and may be executed only by Root under its separate bounded allocation.

## Distinct outcomes

The authoritative state is the last durable snapshot authenticated by its exact bytes and internal payload digest. An accepted move joins that state only through its ordered authority reference and recorded completed accepted attempt. A saved positive move with no adopted reference is an orphan. A raw candidate is checked as a separate state. A saved witness receives an independent direct determinant zero check but never substitutes itself for the durable authority. A saved zero can therefore coexist with an older positive, pending authoritative state after failed publication.

The pure helper reports `authority_chain_passed`, durable score/identity/status, cursor accounting, side-output classifications and `receipt_acceptance_checked: false`. It checks status names, zero/completion consistency and whether a claimed cap is exhausted; it does not establish overall run, validator lifecycle, filesystem durability or resource acceptance. The fixed adapter separately authenticates and associates report, authority, source manifest and owned receipt before returning its own `accepted` result. That result still requires Root's separate audit guard to finish cleanly.

## Independent calculations and limits

For each supplied accepted state, the helper enumerates all point triples with a direct determinant test, checks the grid/row/point invariants, derives the normalized identity and reconstructs I/J/M row and pair priorities. It charges each pending, abandoned, complete and accepted attempt, binds its state/order/cursor/selected rows and verifies cumulative/epoch ceilings. A complete nonimproving record must declare the unchanged score and a structurally legal selected-row candidate; its minimum is not independently optimized or recounted. Accepted artifacts are fully recounted, must strictly improve, must equal their selected-column candidate and must preserve all unselected rows. Acceptance resets both cursors and old coverage; abandoning a pending start retains its charge and does not advance its cursor.

The imported initial row sweep and historical pair prefix remain source-supported prior execution. Directly reconstructing its ranking and verifying later recorded cursor advances does not prove either old or new optimizer minima. The audit is not a neighborhood exclusion or global optimality certificate.

The fixed actual adapter binds the metadata supplied by Root for `data/two-row-suffix75-v1`: final source v4, report, authority, receipt and manifest hashes. It requires the reported no-accept outcome: exactly64 newly completed pair records, old32 prefix, cumulative96 pairs and308 rows,130 authority publications, no pending work, no witness/move references and unchanged fixed65 identity. It directly recounts only one unique state (`max_states=1`) and reconstructs its deterministic ranking. It associates the initially unpinned `initial.json` through its independent fixed-state identity/score, initial counters/order and report handoff, and captures/rechecks its byte hash. All166 original pins, metadata/artifacts, adapter and helper hashes are rechecked before return. No actual artifact was read during adapter preparation.

## Synthetic preparation

The final helper passed13 n4 cases and rejected29 malformed/rehashed cases. Cases include real full-policy zero chains, a genuine positive accepted step, a source-supported imported prefix, pending and explicitly abandoned charged starts, a new nonimproving pair record, saved zero plus an old positive pending authority, a positive orphan not adopted, and a final cap snapshot. Memory publishers and a minimal synthetic witness wrapper are used; these tests make no real-filesystem or dual-validator lifecycle claim. The nonimproving pair completion uses a disclosed current-column result stub; the imported one-pair prefix is synthetic setup rather than evidence of historical execution. All other stated optimizer-derived tiny chains use the preserved v2 worker, whose controller matches final v4 by separate source proof.

Corruptions include value-preserving Boolean substitutions in integer counters/generation/cursors/sequence/prefix/score, wrong state/order identities and rows, charge loss, cursor advance after abandonment, missing/extra/badly hashed move references, wrong minima, incomplete positive results, absent zero witness and invalid status. The complete payload wrapper is rehashed in these tests so rejection cannot be attributed only to the outer digest. Earlier preparation v1 sources and evidence remain preserved. Source inspection found that production final snapshots install `pair_cap` after `drive`; final v2 preparation adds that status and checks actual exhausted allowances. The earlier11-case evidence is not misrepresented as covering final-stop status.
