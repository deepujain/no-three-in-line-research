## Pure helper contract and certificates

All original source indices refer to lexicographically sorted points. Geometry helpers require source order4..74 because the modulo3 theorem is used only where proved.

`direction_inventory(points,n,*,check=None,counters=None)` validates the source, finds maximum same-row and same-column separations H/V with exact original-index witnesses, and exhausts every canonical primitive vector satisfying |a|<=floor(n/H), |b|<=floor(n/V). Canonical sign means first nonzero coordinate positive. Every such vector is admitted with full min/max/width/extreme-index/level-histogram data or rejected with two indices whose projection separation exceeds n. Rejection may stop early once that strict inequality is witnessed; width exactly n remains eligible. Accepted widths must be n-1 or n. The inventory's completion flag is set only after independent certificate reconstruction.

`verify_inventory(points,n,inventory,check,counters)` recomputes H/V, re-enumerates the entire canonical primitive domain, rejects duplicates or omitted vectors, verifies every rejection pair, and recomputes every accepted projection/histogram. It also checks both ordinary axes, the modulo3 injection and the four-direction-class ceiling. Thus completion is supported by witnesses and exact domain coverage rather than counters alone.

`canonical_frames(admitted)` examines at most six unordered row-vector pairs, retains determinant+/-1 and excludes the ordinary axis pair, leaving at most five. `frame_candidates(points,n,frame,check=...)` returns all deficient-axis intersections across all fitting translations, including records marked `occupied` for refusal. Each record has frame, translation, added cell and exact preimage. It verifies inverse/forward identity and enforces at most four raw choices over every placement combined. This helper also supports isolated generic mapped-set fixtures; those tests do not pass or bypass the source gate.

`protected(q,n)` includes every boundary of [-1,n] squared. `analyze_family(points,n,check,counters)` records each raw choice with an explicit occupied/protected/duplicate/eligible reason, deduplicates exterior q while retaining a concrete witness, and checks each against old pairs. It stores and rechecks one original collinear-pair obstruction per rejected q. Survivors require all source-pair tests. The result separates `inventory_complete`, `candidates_complete`, `pairs_complete`, `family_complete` and `certificate_complete`; a partial inventory or partial pair loop cannot conclude anything positive or negative. `build_candidate` checks determinant, protected-box refusal and forward identity before reconstructing the target.

The report preserves the entire bounded inventory, raw frame choices, exclusion reasons, eligible witnesses, pair certificates, completion flags and counters. An empty nonordinary-frame or exterior-candidate set can close this family only after complete inventory/candidate verification. At most11,100 direction certificates are retained. Vector coefficients fit signed bytes and source indices fit unsigned bytes, but this implementation deliberately uses readable bounded dictionaries/tuples. Each rejected record holds one vector and two indices. Accepted directions retain at most75 level/count pairs each; only four canonical directions survive.
