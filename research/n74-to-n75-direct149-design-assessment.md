## Exact reduction and limited rationale

Let B be an authenticated valid148-point74-square construction. Every old row and column contains exactly two points. An unchanged translation into the75-square has offset(a,b) in{0,1}². All74 translated source rows and columns remain saturated. Therefore an additional point must lie simultaneously in the single unused row and single unused column: the unique cell

    z_(a,b) = (74 if a=0 else0, 74 if b=0 else0).

Each translation has exactly one axis-feasible addition. The entire unchanged-source149 family has four candidates, not a75² candidate scan or a matching/search problem. Each candidate has the correct149-point axis pattern, including an occupied deficient intersection. Since B is valid and ordinary translation preserves its geometry, the candidate is valid exactly when z is noncollinear with every pair of translated old points. Ordinary rotations/reflections give isometric versions of these cases, not a reason to allocate another orientation loop.

This is a source-independent cardinality/axis reduction and a new selected-source question relative to the completed76→75 contractions. It starts from a valid148 set and does not alter its old geometry. In contrast, the completed contraction family had a concrete obstruction at every cut; its reported minimum173 was not independently recounted. That contrast motivates a cheap finite check, **not** a claim that any of the four new corners is likely to be clear. The previously closed unchanged n78 extensions concern another source/order and do not mathematically exclude these n74 candidates. The generic extension mechanism is old; no algorithmic novelty or advantage over an already completed identical n74 check is claimed.

## Symmetry shortcut that must be rejected

An odd-cardinality set of more than one point cannot be both no-three-in-line and invariant under a half-turn about any center. Every noncentral orbit has two points; odd cardinality forces the center into the set. There must then be an antipodal pair, which is collinear with that center. This immediately excludes exact half-turn, quarter-turn and full dihedral invariance for149 points on75, and also for157 on79. It is a general elementary obstruction, not a new large-grid result. Reflection alone is not excluded by this argument.

Consequently, a proposed centered-gap expansion of a centrally symmetric74 source followed by insertion of the new grid center is doomed without any numerical test. Expanding each coordinate x to x+[x>=37] maps the old reflection x→73−x to the new reflection x→74−x. The148 mapped points remain centrally symmetric; adding(37,37) creates a center/antipodal triple. The tiny2→3 analogue is the four corners of a3-square plus its center, which contains both diagonal triples. The four ordinary translations above do not introduce that symmetry and are not excluded by this argument.

Recommendation: retain only the conditional four-candidate unchanged-source check while source/prior-scope questions are resolved. Do not convert it into a broad direct149 walk, a new symmetric SAT family, or a repair after the first obstruction. If the four cases are already known or later all certified blocked, this design supplies no supported continuation.
