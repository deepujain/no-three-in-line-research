# From-scratch n=75 row-block breakout: independent mathematical and overlap review

Decision: PASS for implementation and synthetic testing only, conditional on engineering/resource review. Reviewed `research/from-scratch-row-block-breakout75-design.md` SHA-256 `23a31e84ead3756cbcc367ccf2f650cee0695519345f43a729ae07924e2fe5b3`. No actual geometry or search was performed. This is an incomplete heuristic, not a claim of effectiveness or algorithmic novelty.

After removing a row, no retained point lies on its horizontal line. Two distinct added points in that row therefore cannot form a mixed triple with a retained point, and at most two additions cannot form an all-new triple. For each added point, summing positive line weight times C(t,2) over retained line occupancies counts its new triples exactly, including multiplicities when a line has four or more points. Hence the two unary costs add exactly. The separation does not apply to a two-row deficiency migration, which needs its specified sequential exact delta. Every represented state has 149 distinct points with one row of load one and all others of load two; a genuine valid 149-point set has that row pattern. A zero unweighted triple score excludes vertical overload as well as all other collinear triples, but must still be independently validated.

Source-level comparison with `src/trade_search.py`: that worker represents full 2n states as two disjoint permutations, requires degree two on both axes, uses two/three-row rotations or rectangle switches, and supports temperature, recoloring, restarts, focus and optional tabu. The prior n=79 157-point focused tabu started from a specific invalid source and preserved both axis capacities. The proposed n=75 search starts independently, keeps only row capacity hard, permits column violations, replaces a full row by the minimum weighted distinct choice, and persistently changes weights. It explores a different represented family and policy; this is not proof of better performance or disjointness from every earlier heuristic. Existing matching/growth and fixed-source insertion families also do not exhaust this state space.

Adaptive violated-constraint weighting is established: [Morris's 1993 Breakout paper](https://cse.unl.edu/~choueiry/Documents/BreakoutMorris1993.pdf) describes increasing weights of currently matched nogoods to escape local minima. The proposed fixed 32-stagnation update and unconditional sampled moves are a specific policy, not a claim to have invented weighting or breakout. A previously reported [n=75 local-search attempt](https://einsteinarena.com/problems/no-three-in-line-75/threads/271) did not find a 149-point witness, but its public description is insufficient to establish exhaustive overlap with this exact policy or an absence of other prior results.

Before any pilot, the stated synthetic falsifiers must establish full weighted-score and migration deltas, correct distinct tie selection, no partial sample/update on interruption, atomic penalty caps, exact zero-witness preservation after validator two, and final pin/deadline/cleanup rejection. Full index, 65,536-key weight table and telemetry worst-case memory need measurement under the owned guard; finite operation counts are not a runtime or RSS guarantee. Accepted pilot telemetry must distinguish unweighted improvements, movement and migrations from weighted-objective changes. A positive-score state is invalid, and a timeout or failed search excludes no configurations. A later continuation needs its own reviewed state/weights/RNG contract and separate allocation.
