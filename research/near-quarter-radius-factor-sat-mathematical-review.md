# Exact residual radius degree feasibility via SAT

Decision: mathematically approve separate pure-helper and shadow-wrapper source/tiny development. This review launches no solver and authorizes no host measurement, traversal, packed genesis or frozen-frontier changes.

Every geometric completion chooses a subset of the complete individually admissible orbit edges and has exact radius degrees d(v). Use one Boolean variable for each distinct edge instance, including parallel/oriented orbit variables, and constrain the sum of incident variables to equal d(v) for every radius. An UNSAT result on a complete, correctly encoded mask proves no geometric completion. A SAT result establishes only degree feasibility; decode its edge variables and independently check the degrees. It is not a geometric witness. Zero-demand endpoints can either force all incident variables false or be removed consistently. Handle degree<d and empty incidence explicitly. Use one shared auxiliary ID pool beyond all edge variables.

This is strictly stronger than the earlier component/degree and two/three-vertex cut conditions. Consider a hub joined by one edge to each of five disjoint triangles, with every one of its 16 vertices demanding degree one. There are 20 edges. Degrees suffice, the graph is connected and nonbipartite, and total demand is even. Total demand 16 makes all proposed two/three-vertex cuts redundant by the established dominance argument. Nevertheless no exact factor exists: deleting the hub leaves five odd components, while a matching can use at most one hub edge. Thus the SAT relaxation can add information before the late-only cut threshold. This abstract example is not claimed to arise from a geometric prefix.

For n79 the quotient has 39 positive radii and a central radius. Distinct positive-radius pairs support at most two oriented edges, and each central-positive pair supports one, so E<=39*38+39=1521. Positive vertex degree is at most 77 and central degree at most 39. Demands are at most two, with central demand at most one. Sum of incidence counts is at most 3042.

The installed PySAT CardEnc.equals implementation combines AtLeast and AtMost sequential counters; its source was inspected. Pure finite encoding arithmetic, without a SAT solver, checked every q=0..77 and every d=0..min(2,q), 231 cases. For d=0 it uses q unit clauses. For d=1 and q>=3 it uses q-1 auxiliaries, 3q-3 clauses and 7q-8 literal occurrences. For d=2 and q>=4 it uses 4(q-2) auxiliaries, 8(q-2) clauses and 19q-40 literal occurrences. Small exceptional arities were checked directly. Consequently conservative full-mask bounds are 12168 auxiliary variables, 13689 total variables, 24336 clauses and 57798 literal occurrences. These are encoding-size bounds, not native RSS/runtime measurements.
