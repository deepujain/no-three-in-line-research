Exactly 35 selected full four-point off-diagonal orbits plus one selected two-point diagonal orbit expand to precisely the 142 decoded points. `model_selected_orbit_ids` records the 36 variables and the deterministic orbit-map SHA-256 is `82c1fcb850886b2d837860512a29ec7274da5739d480bb8b07ac460e76cb019b`.
