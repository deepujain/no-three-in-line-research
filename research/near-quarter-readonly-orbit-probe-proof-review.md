# Independent exact read-only orbit admission proof

Approved for an isolated tiny prototype under Root's explicit authorization. Reviewed design `research/near-quarter-readonly-orbit-probe-proposal.md` SHA-256 `a1bcdfb62af20310a04c9e8b2c19d24dfb9f301f1f1ccc509a9a7d0691d4476d`, together with current packed-state push/blocker semantics. No production source, active manifest, host process or broad-grid geometry was changed or launched by this review.

Let S be the coherent valid current selected set and C the candidate orbit, with all candidate cells distinct and unoccupied. Every triple in their union contains zero, one, two or three points of C. Zero-candidate triples are absent by validity of S. For one-candidate triples, the existing blocker count at that candidate cell is zero exactly when no selected pair has a secant through it. For two-candidate triples, traversing the maximal grid line through that candidate pair and testing occupied cells detects every selected point on it. Candidate endpoints are unoccupied, so they introduce no false rejection. Integer determinants check all candidate-only triples. These cases exhaust all possible collinear triples, giving an exact predicate rather than a relaxation.

The query must also preserve strict orbit-ID, selected/family and capacity checks, reject repeated candidate cells before pair-line construction, and compare complete candidate row/column contributions plus existing loads against two. Unusable, poisoned or latched states must fail as errors; they must not be converted into ordinary inadmissibility. Equivalence to successful push/undo is claimed for coherent valid states, not identical mutation/poison semantics on corrupt internal capacity states. Committed pushes and final independent verifiers remain unchanged.

The candidate contributes at most six pair lines and four internal triples. Only one bounded line span need be held. Budget checks before and within loops must propagate interruption without returning a partial admission verdict. Read-only means geometry and transaction metadata are unchanged; any physical counters still modified must retain clearly documented actual-work semantics. Blocker-increment and inverse-write counters cannot count read queries.

Prototype tests should exhaust tiny coherent prefixes and candidates against current push/full undo and independent determinant checking, cover duplicate/axis/old-pair/new-pair/internal-triple cases, and interrupt every query boundary with unchanged geometry snapshots. Integration additionally requires equality of masks, histograms, MRV/cursor behavior, replay and live state under a new reviewed source identity. This design approval neither changes the frozen current sources nor establishes n79 speedup or permits a larger numerical allocation.
