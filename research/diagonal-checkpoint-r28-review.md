Among square symmetries preserving main-diagonal reflection, simultaneous reversal `(x,y) -> (78-x,78-y)` maps `(d,t)` to `(78-d,78-t)`; composing with transpose has the same effect on invariant sets. This is an elementary symmetry comparison, not a claim that every other potential transformation has been classified.
