Author: Mathematical Reviewer, `gpt-6-astra`, high. Source/tiny adaptation only, awaiting independent guard review. This adds `research/diagonal_residual_checkpoint_guard.py` and its focused tests; frozen reflection core, guard, chain, shared dispatcher and owned-process helper are unchanged. The residual core remains the independently approved `d8576102cbd904a23a6170807e89df9d4ab5610bdc1acb88351a7f8d6660ce6c`.

The guard is a narrow adaptation of frozen guard `9b84eb89e009d32a2daa3cdfa6b3b5df9d63daa48ff2565c6f4572a682fb5d2f`. Changes are closed residual imports and source keys, the residual schema and prune policy, the standalone file as its own entrypoint identity, its descriptive text and temporary-directory prefix. The exact accepted identity is `diagonal-residual-rowbatch-frontier-v1`, `minimum-viable-row-then-lexicographic-unordered-batch-v1` and `residual-row-degree-component-parity-bipartite-sum-v1`, together with grid size, sorted slices, orbit-order hash and residual core sources. It does not accept, relabel or migrate an old reflection checkpoint. There is no new dispatcher or chain route.

The inherited supervisor records the invocation clock before imports in standalone use, holds the exclusive checkpoint lock, requires an external predecessor hash and delegates only a private staged checkpoint to the unchanged owned-process helper. Limits remain at most 30 seconds and 256 MiB. The reserved cooperative interval, per-invocation witness directory, pre/post source checks, two independent integer witness verifiers, atomic publication and rejection rules remain unchanged. A failed reconstruction reporting an unchanged checkpoint and zero new nodes is `reconstruction_timeout`, without promotion. A late source change, cancellation or wall crossing clears the accepted hash even when published bytes remain; `published_checkpoint_sha256` preserves this distinction. Publication and final filesystem writes have the inherited late-rejection caveat, not a new hard real-time I/O guarantee. This adapter retains the mutable-checkpoint contract and does not introduce an immutable accepted-HEAD protocol.

The core's `physical_work` restore/traversal groups and `restore_seconds`, `traversal_seconds`, `publication_seconds` pass through in `worker_result` as diagnostics. They do not change checkpoint logical counters, and are not a measurement of the whole supervisor invocation. Certificate method metadata is `diagonal_residual_checkpoint` and guard source metadata binds the independent witness publication to this standalone adapter.

The tests replace the monitor with a synchronous tiny subprocess runner, so they are correctness and fault-injection evidence, not actual-host sampled RSS or owned-tree cleanup evidence. They exercise standalone CLI fresh/resume, exact source/entrypoint identity and cap validation; repeated tiny resumes matching independent residual-reference logical counts and witness sets; old schema/policy/source rejection; unchanged reconstruction timeout; oversized/hash/lock rejection; worker/resource/monitor/cleanup/cancel failure; dual-verified witness preservation apart from rejected frontier; identity tampering; before/after publication faults; and late source/cancel/wall failures during checkpoint and final-report publication.
