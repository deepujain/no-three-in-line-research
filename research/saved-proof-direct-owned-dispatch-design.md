## Proposed distinct dispatch architecture

Use the unchanged monotonic checker SHA `09e85dced3d7cf572cad4b75f9b4fab50dd051f4fe9f95ded58981de6ded5c90` as the sole owned worker, through a new small stdlib-only direct entrypoint. Do not invoke `native.run_child`, an inner tracker, an inner snapshot, another Python worker or the old saved-proof wrapper. The existing `owned_process_guard.guarded_process` launches the direct worker as its owned session/group root. Its observer remains a child of the guard outside that owned group. This removes both duplicate polling and the specific inner-observer sibling-attribution path.

The new direct worker begins a four-second cooperative interval at module entry, performs reviewed source/interpreter/exact-saved-pair pin checks, imports only the unchanged checker plus stdlib, and calls `check_pair` exactly once with `seconds=4` and the original entry timestamp. All checker limits and canonical deletion semantics remain unchanged. The enclosing **unchanged owned guard uses5 seconds and256MiB**, leaving a scheduling reserve for publication/cleanup. No mathematical criterion, input, proof line or clause is changed. This is a newly reviewed dispatch/allocation, not an automatic retry of the failed wrapper.

The owned guard remains sampled and may overshoot during observation/cleanup. Its existing elapsed-time check must reject any run that fails its five-second acceptance bound; do not describe it as an instantaneous kernel timer. A cooperative four-second deadline is a reserve, not a completion guarantee. Root must account for actual wall usage even on failure.

Before reading the proof, the direct worker verifies the immutable source manifest and exact saved hashes. Its checker then independently preflights and fully parses/hashes the pair as before. It emits one bounded JSON receipt to the guard's new regular log file. Reject a cap-reaching log, malformed/incomplete JSON, unexpected exit, missing checked empty, hash mismatch, incomplete parsing, failed final pins, or any non-clean owned receipt. Normal checker failure details retain the first failing line/clause.
