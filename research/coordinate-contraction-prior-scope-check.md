# Coordinate contraction: focused prior-scope check

Checked 27 November 2026; source reading only, no numerical search or contact. This is not a novelty survey.

The [Grey Strand research receipts, nos. 294 and 297](https://thegreystrand.com/office/) describe an n=77 compression attempt based on the published n=78 full configuration. Receipt 294 says all 6,084 choices of one row and one column were deleted and reports only four clean boundary crops, each with 152 points. Receipt 297 reports an independent replay of the 6,084 cuts and a bounded repair attempt from the four crops, with best valid count 152 and no 154 found. Receipt 294 labels the work a compression experiment; its public summary does not specify the exact interior-coordinate remapping or expose the cited vault code. The complete 78×78 choice count and description strongly indicate overlap with arbitrary row/column contraction, including interior choices; exact equivalence of the implementations remains unverified. Do not describe the n=78 to n=77 contraction as distinct from this reported experiment without inspecting its code. The repair search's failure is not an impossibility proof.

The same receipts do not describe an n=76 to n=75 contraction experiment. That absence does not establish novelty or exclude prior work elsewhere. Even for the proposed occupied-intersection method, surviving axis capacities follow from its combinatorics, while Euclidean noncollinearity must be separately checked after compression; the existence of a 149- or 153-point configuration cannot be inferred from the deletion count alone.

[Flammenkamp's table](https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/table.html), dated 28 October 2026, lists full 2n configurations for n=74, 76 and 78 and no full entry for n=75 or 77. Embedding the listed 74 and 76 configurations yields valid lower bounds of 148 for n=75 and 152 for n=77, respectively; the axis argument gives upper bounds 150 and 154. The cited receipt additionally reports four 152-point n=77 crops. These are attributable lower bounds and elementary upper bounds, not a claim that 148 and 152 are the best known globally. No independent witness verification was done in this scope check.
