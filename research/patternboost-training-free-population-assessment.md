# Training-free shrink-and-saturate population: assessment

Decision: **do not implement or allocate this as a new numerical branch now**. This is a design/literature assessment only: no order-79 domain construction, scoring, search, training, new dependency or host allocation was performed. The published PatternBoost method includes a learned sequence model; simply keeping a pool of greedy-repaired states without that model should not be described as a reproduction of it.

## What already exists

`src/search.py` starts each repair from its single valid incumbent, randomly deletes up to a configured maximum, shuffles vacant cells, and greedily inserts exact zero-conflict candidates. The independently audited 25-second maximum-12 deletion baseline reported 102 passes, 617,216 candidate evaluations and no improvement from the embedded 156-point seed; actual deletion sizes and per-pass diversity were not recorded (`research/seeded-greedy-delete12-baseline-review.md`).

`research/neutral_repair.py` already retains a separate valid walk state, greedily repairs 6–12 deletions, permits neutral and bounded downhill transitions, and stores a bounded hash cache. The audited neutral-only sample reported 76 identical returns in 77 completed passes and no distinct equal-size retained state (`research/neutral-repair-seed279106-review.md`). Later valley runs retained valid 154/155-point states without improving the separately kept 156-point best (`data/overnight-valley-batch-root-verification.json`). These are bounded observations, not exhaustion, and do not prove a larger destruction or a different selection policy cannot work.

A finite top-k population, larger destruction size, random selection among retained parents, and seeded resaturation would alter state management and the sampled trajectory, but its core step remains the already tested delete-and-greedy-repair mechanism. The extra retained states do not themselves produce new candidates or prove useful diversity; without a learned generator or a separately justified crossover, calling it a new mechanism would overstate the difference. A scoring-only top-k pool can also crowd out structurally different smaller states, while expensive independent verification for every entrant reduces the limited useful work. Those are design risks, not measured runtime claims.

## Distance and resources are not established by a pool

Using a larger temporary deletion count is not an effective source-distance guarantee: greedy saturation can reinsert deleted cells. Keeping a point in a child that is absent from one parent also does not guarantee distance from either fixed source. To guarantee a target-157 child lies outside the invalid 158-point source's already tested deletion ranges through 12, every completed child must explicitly satisfy |S minus child| >= 13; analogous claims about published-seed repairs require their own effective-deletion calculation and exact family scope. Rejecting near-source outputs only at the end may spend nearly all budget rediscovering rejected states; forbidding original cells on reinsertion would define a stronger and different restricted family, and must be reflected in its exact target contract.

The one separately proposed [source-disjoint matching rebuild](source-disjoint-matching-rebuild157-proposal.md) already establishes a stronger structural separation: any 157-point completion has at least 80 deletions from S, with a valid-state secant check and a necessary matching gate. It is currently awaiting its own source/tiny review, so a population of the same basic destructive-greedy operations is not supported as a higher-priority parallel allocation merely by the PatternBoost analogy. The matching branch's future result does not prove a population cannot succeed.

## What would change the decision

A future proposal needs a specific, reviewable generation rule that differs from repeatedly shrinking one parent and greedily refilling it; two verified parent identities and an exact crossover or other novel state transition; direct effective-distance enforcement against each claimed source scope; a bounded pool and replacement rule that retains explicit diversity rather than only high scores; and deterministic tiny-board oracle tests of geometry and invariant accounting. It also needs an independently reviewed cost and one explicit 25-second soft / 30-second owned / sampled 256-MiB gate, including exact partial-stop behavior and independent validation of any saved witness. A defensible distinction or evidence of useful diversity must precede implementation and allocation. No claim is made that the existing prior methods or this rejected idea exhaust the problem.
